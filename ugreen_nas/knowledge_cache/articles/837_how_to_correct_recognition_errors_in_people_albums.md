# How to Correct Recognition Errors in People Albums

> **Article ID**: `837`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Photos > FAQ > How to Correct Recognition Errors in People Albums`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `COMMON`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/837  

---

After enabling people recognition, the system automatically groups photos based on facial features. If any photos are incorrectly recognized, you can manually remove and reassign them to improve the accuracy of your People albums.

## PC

**Steps:**

In the Photos app on PC, open a person album. If you find incorrectly grouped photos, you can correct them in the following ways:

**Method 1: Single-photo correction**

1. Locate the misclassified photo in the person album, right-click the photo.

2. In the menu, choose "**Move to another person**" to assign it to the correct group, or "**Remove from this person**" to simply ungroup it from the current person.

**Method 2: Batch correction**

1. Hover over the photos you want to fix and click the checkbox that appears to select multiple images.

2. After selection, a toolbar will appear at the bottom. Click the "**...**" button on the right.

3. Choose "**Move to another person**" or "**Remove from this person**" to complete the batch correction.

## Mobile

**Steps:**

After entering "**Categorization**" and opening the relevant People album, you can correct errors in two ways:

**Method 1: Batch correction**

1. While viewing a People album, long-press photos to select multiple incorrectly recognized images.

2. Once selected, a floating action bar will appear at the bottom of the screen.

3. Scroll the bottom bar and tap "**Remove**".

4. In the pop-up options, choose "**Move to another person**" or "**Remove from this person**" to complete batch correction.

**Method 2: Single-photo correction**

1. Tap a misidentified photo to enter its detail page.

2. Once selected, a floating action bar will appear at the bottom.

3. Scroll the bottom bar and tap "**Remove**".

4. Choose "**Move to another person**" or "**Remove from this person**" to correct it.
