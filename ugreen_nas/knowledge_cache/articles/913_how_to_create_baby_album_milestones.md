# How to Create Baby Album Milestones?

The screenshots in this document are for reference only. The actual interface may vary slightly depending on the system or app version, please refer to the actual interface displayed on your device.

## Introduction

**Baby Album Milestones** is a collection of photos that records important anniversaries (such as birth and the first month) or special events (such as the first steps and the first trip) during your baby’s growth. The system can **intelligently generate** Milestones based on your baby’s date of birth, and also allows you to **create them manually**, helping you organize and preserve precious memories of your baby’s growth.

## Before You Start

● Make sure you have created a Baby Album in the "**Photos**" app.

● Make sure the Baby Album contains at least 3 photos that meet the requirements.

● To use the automatic generation feature, make sure complete baby information (such as nickname and date of birth) has been added to the Baby Album.

## Steps

You can create Baby Album Milestones in multiple ways.

### Method 1: Generate Milestones Intelligently

The system intelligently identifies important milestones (such as birth, one month old, 100 days old, etc.) based on your baby’s date of birth, and selects photos from the Baby Album to generate Milestones.

**Path**: Open the "**Baby Album**" details page and click "**Milestones**". The system will automatically scan photos in the Baby Album and intelligently generate Milestones based on your baby’s date of birth.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-28/90548e0f7ea945e386f19f4c02c45d35.webp)

**Note**: The time required for intelligent organization depends on the number of photos in the Baby Album. After new Milestones are generated, a notification badge will appear on the page, and you can click "**Play as slideshow**" to preview them.

### Method 2: Create Milestones Manually

If you want to create Milestones for specific themes (such as "Baby’s First Time Sitting Up"), you can create them manually.

1. Open the "**Baby Album**" details page and click "**Milestones**".

● For the first creation: Click "**Create**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-28/4a2f8c3ec49949dd824812c898ebe30e.webp)

● If Milestones already exist: Click "**Create** **milestone**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-28/1497d0d6eec14ac89b7912e51b37e103.webp)

2. Enter the Milestone name (such as "Baby Can Sit Up Independently"). The system will intelligently find relevant photos from the Baby Album for quick selection.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-28/d4c6b1b652904b7aaa505d14d189be49.webp)

3. Select the date range for the Milestone. Photos will be arranged and displayed on the Milestones page according to this timeline.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-28/d92ef5a1616a4f2cbdfe1c67176e76cc.webp)

4. Select photos from the Baby Album, or add photos from computer/Nas library, then click "**Create** **milestone**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-28/45263c32826648e8a6274bc544665e37.webp)

After creation, you can view and manage the Milestone on the "**Milestones**"details page.

### Method 3: Create Milestones from Selected Photos

When browsing the Baby Album, you can select multiple photos to quickly create Milestones.

1. Open the Baby Album details page, select the photos you want to add to Milestones, and click "**Add to Milestones**" from the "**...**" menu.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-28/a6e8afff11a64064b3e933b9c793fe97.webp)

2. Select "**Create milestone**" or add the photos to an existing Milestone.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-28/bdb06086664246b589d9490150fec381.webp)

3. If you choose to create a new Milestone, enter the Milestone name. After returning to the Milestones list, you can view the newly created Milestone. To modify the Milestone date, open the individual Milestone details page and edit it.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-28/26e09c35bfb34e80ba895fcd40dc0b52.webp)

## View and Manage Milestones

### View Milestones

Return to the Milestones list to view all Milestones arranged by date timeline.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-28/c3efdaf78a8143619a42668d4774dbd7.webp)

### Manage Milestones

On the Milestones list, the following operations are supported:

● **Create milestone**:Continue adding more Milestones.

● **Play as slideshow**: Select a single Milestone to play as a slideshow.

● **Like**: Like a single Milestone.

On an individual Milestone details page, the following operations are supported:

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-28/0e4fcc06cff44e179eb2ab23556bfed0.webp)

● **Like and comment**: View interactions from family members or like and comment on Milestones created by others.

● **Upload photos**: Manually add photos to the Milestone.

● **Rename**: Modify the Milestone name.

● **Play as slideshow**: Preview the photo collection in the Milestone.

● **Edit milestone date**: Modify the date range of the Milestone.

● **Delete milestone**: Delete the selected Milestone.

**Note**: Administrators have all operation permissions by default. The operation permissions of non-administrator users are assigned by administrators in the sharing settings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: What types of Milestones can be generated intelligently?**

The system intelligently generates two types of Milestones based on your baby’s date of birth:

● **Important date milestones**: Such as birth, one month old, 100 days old, first birthday, and yearly birthdays.

● **Holiday and festival milestones**: Such as New Year’s Day, Spring Festival, Lantern Festival, Children’s Day, Mid-Autumn Festival, National Day, Christmas, etc.

**Note**:

● Each date is recommended only once. If there are more than 30 photos, the system randomly selects photos.

● After you modify your baby’s date of birth, the system will regenerate age-related Milestones based on the new date.

● Recognition of overseas holidays depends on the location information of when photos were taken.

● Holiday aggregation generates a "First xx" title only for the first corresponding holiday after your baby’s birth.

**Q2: Is there a limit to the number of intelligently generated Milestones?**

● The Milestones list supports up to **2,000** Milestones. When the limit is reached, the system will display a notification.

● There is no limit on the number of photos in a single Milestone.

**Q3: What are the photo requirements for intelligently generated Milestones?**

● **Quantity**: Each Milestone requires at least 3 photos and supports up to 30 photos. If there are more than 30 photos, the system randomly selects photos.

● **Size**: Each photo must be larger than 300 KB.

● **Type**: Supports photos, videos, and Live Photos.

● **Source**: Only photos in the current Baby Album are supported (excluding photos marked as sensitive protected).

● **Deduplication**: If the "**Similar & Duplicate Photo Recognition**" model is enabled, the system automatically removes duplicates and keeps only one copy of identical photos.

**Q4: When are intelligently generated Milestones created?**

● **Trigger timing**: When you enter the Baby Album, the system scans and **aggregates photos every 2 hours**.

● **Notification**: When new Milestones are generated, a pop-up notification appears in the Baby Album. If multiple Milestones are generated at the same time, only the latest one is displayed, and each device shows the pop-up once.

● **Viewing record**: The system records viewed Milestones, unviewed Milestones display a badge.

**Q5: What happens to generated Milestones after modifying the baby’s date of birth?**

After modifying the baby’s date of birth, the system will reorganize the Milestone timeline based on the new date and refresh the page. All previously generated Milestones will be retained.

**Q6: Why do recommended Milestones need to be generated based on the baby’s date of birth?**

The system associates photo information (such as shooting time and holiday dates) with your baby’s date of birth to provide accurate Milestone recommendations, such as "Baby’s First Spring Festival" or "Baby’s First Birthday". At the same time, by identifying your baby’s age, the system can filter photos from the corresponding growth stages and avoid recommending photos unrelated to your baby’s development.
