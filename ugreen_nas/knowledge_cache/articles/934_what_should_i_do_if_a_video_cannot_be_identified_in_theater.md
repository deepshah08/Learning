# What Should I Do If a Video Cannot Be Identified in Theater?

> **Article ID**: `934`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Theater > FAQ > What Should I Do If a Video Cannot Be Identified in Theater?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`

## Applicability

**Applicable Clients:** UGREEN NAS PC Client (Windows/macOS), Web Browser

**Applicable Version:** UGOS Pro firmware 1.13.0.0059 or later

The descriptions in this document are for reference only. The actual interface may vary slightly depending on the system or app version. Please refer to the actual interface.

## Issue Description

If the video is self-produced or its file name does not follow a recognizable naming format, Theater may be unable to find the corresponding film information. You may encounter the following issues:

● The film poster cannot be matched.

● The film description is not displayed.

● Cast and crew information is missing.

● The film information is identified incorrectly.

● The video is matched to the wrong film.

Try the following methods.

## Method 1: Edit Film Information Manually

Self-produced videos, home videos, course videos, and similar content usually cannot be matched with film information from the film metadata source. In this case, you can add the film information manually.

1. Open Theater, click the target video poster, and go to the video details page.

2. Click "**···**" at the top > "**Edit information**", then manually add the film information.

3. After making your changes, click "**Save**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-03/51e6e2fa888048d8ac88103fe533405c.webp)

## Method 2: Identify Film Information Manually

If the video can be matched with film metadata but the system fails to identify it automatically, you can identify the film information manually from the video details page.

1. Open Theater, click the target video poster, and go to the video details page.

2. Click "**···**" at the top>"**Scan manually**", enter the correct film title, and search.

Follow the on-screen instructions to search for and select the correct film. Once selected, the system will update the film information for the video, including the film poster, description, cast and crew information, and more.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-03/89c077ec11c04e598d47e5e0a0a1728d.webp)

## Method 3: Rename the File and Match Again

If the video file name does not follow a recognizable naming format, Theater may not identify the film information correctly. Rename the file using a more recognizable film title, then rescan or match the media library again.

You can include the official film title, release year, season, and episode information in the file name. For example: The.Mirror.2019.S01E01. For naming conventions, see "[Theater Movie Naming Convention Guidelines](https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/detail/article/en-US/257) ".

After renaming the file, rescan or match the media library again so the system can identify the film information.
