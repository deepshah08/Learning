## Overview

**Voice Memos** is an AI-powered voice note application developed exclusively for UGREEN NAS. Centered on audio, it integrates advanced speech recognition models and large language models. With one-click recording, it quickly converts speech into text and offers practical features such as multi-template intelligent summaries, mind maps, and translation. It is an ideal assistant for meeting minutes, academic learning, and capturing inspiration.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/bc3c46316ba041c18fe534eefe095d05.webp)

## Installation and Access

1. Open the "**App Center**" and locate the "**Voice Memos**" application.

2. Click "**Install**" and follow the on-screen instructions to complete the setup.

3. Once installed, click the "**Voice Memos**" icon on the desktop or in "**My apps"** to start using it.

## Recording and Importing

Voice Memos supports both live recording and importing existing audio files.

### Live Recording (Available on PC and Mobile Devices)

After enabling microphone permissions on your recording device (such as a computer), click "**Start recording**" to begin. During recording, you can **pause**, **resume**, or **add real-time notes to keypoints** (real-time noting is supported on the PC version only).

#### Adding Key Notes

Add key notes during recording. The AI will generate highlighted summaries based on these notes, allowing you to quickly review important segments and create enhanced summaries.

**Two types of real-time notes are supported:**

● **Text Notes**: Enter text descriptions during recording (the content may be left blank). The system will automatically record the current timestamp. Each note is limited to 150 words.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/af3c90b16ea64d54bcf331d8c8335cd1.webp)

● **Quick Notes**: Click the "**Note**" button to create a blank note with a timestamp, which you can fill in later.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/1bf90f8331dd45c5a1aded7e620162c1.webp)

#### Viewing and Managing Notes

After recording, click "**View note**" to see all timestamps and descriptions in the right-side panel. The following actions are supported:

● Click a timestamp to jump directly to the corresponding position in the recording. If transcription is available, the system will automatically locate the corresponding text segment.

● Double-click the text to edit the content of the note.

● Add multiple notes at the same timestamp.

● Delete timestamps and their associated notes.

**Note**: A single audio file supports up to 126 notes.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/60b8916e3e37475aa508bb62fcaee08f.webp)

### Importing Recordings

You can directly import locally stored audio files for recognition and processing.

## AI Recognition and Generation

After completing a live recording or importing an audio file, you can generate transcriptions, intelligent summaries, and mind maps with a single click.

### Enabling AI Models

When using Voice Memos for the first time, you need to download and enable the AI models.

● **Enable the speech recognition model**: After authorization, it accurately converts speech into clear text with one click, eliminating the need for repeated playback and making conversations easy to review.

● **Enable the LLM**: After authorization, it intelligently extracts key insights from recordings and generates summaries and mind maps, helping you focus on essential information.

### Transcription

Based on the selected language and template, the system intelligently identifies speakers and automatically generates text.

On the "**Transcript**" page, you can:

● Click the menu in the upper-right corner to search, translate, regenerate transcript, or export the transcription.

● Click "**Speaker**" to batch-edit speaker names.

● Double-click the text to edit or copy the content.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/ab98f762f9704c59bfda2009cb789596.webp)

### Intelligent Summary

Summaries are automatically generated based on the selected template (such as meetings or reports).

On the "**Summary**" page, you can:

● Click the menu in the upper-right corner to edit, translate, regenerate, or export the summary.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/c79df96df2f74327b61af67d1a70abfa.webp)

● Customize summary templates by clicking "**Regenerate summary**">"**Create template**". The AI will generate a new summary based on your custom template.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/64c8c456d632441cae57a6e155b03d98.webp)

**Note**: If the transcription is too short, the system may be unable to generate a summary.

### Mind Map

Mind maps are automatically generated based on the summary content.

On the "**Mind map**" page, the following operations are supported:

● Click the "**...**" icon in the upper-right corner to export the mind map.

● Zoom in, zoom out, or adjust the display to fit the page while viewing.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/32bc618e6094484d9af60ba7c46bbfab.webp)

### Multilingual Translation

Recognized text and summaries support one-click translation. Currently, only Chinese, English, German, and Japanese are supported for mutual translation.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/b7a42d6cdebf4d0c8cdcb272ef95bcf5.webp)

## Playback, Proofreading, and Editing

After recognition is complete, you can refine and organize the content with precision.

● **Playback Controls**: Supports playback of the original recording for proofreading alongside the text. The player supports 10s fast-forward and rewind, as well as playback speeds ranging from 0.5× to 2×.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/4fcc75af9f6c4433be8b81dfaeb30ccd.webp)

● **Text Editing**: Double-click the text or click "**Edit**"icon in the upper-right corner to manually correct transcription errors.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/9725eb2132ca4695b90143a1f7b74e10.webp)

● **Search and Replace**: Supports full-document keyword searches and batch replacement, enabling quick and consistent modifications.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/1595c15e74114cc3b6c16d3a104921bd.webp)

## Recording File Management

You can organize and manage all recordings through grouping, filtering, sorting, exporting, and deletion.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/b6786e59df29473e9a8e8d1e4747e299.webp)

● **Add and Export**: Click "**Add recording**" to import multiple audio files from local storage. Click the "**...**" icon in the upper-right corner to export recordings.

● **Create Groups**: Click "**Show groups"** in the upper-right corner to create new groups. You can drag recordings directly into a group or right-click and select "**Move to**".

● **Filter and Sort**: The recording list on the left supports filtering and sorting by "**transcription status**", "**grouping status**", "**added time**", and "**updated time**".

● **Search**:Quickly locate recordings using keyword search.

● **Quick Actions**: Right-click a recording to access options such as "**Rename**", "**Move to**", "**Open file location**", and "**Delete**".

## Settings

Click the "**Settings**" icon in the lower-left corner to manage the storage location of recordings and configure general upload policies.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/ff266f4a1d8e4d5ba740bd4aa4e2e1e6.webp)

### File Location Settings

On the "**File location**" page, you can view and manage the storage paths of recordings on the NAS.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/0642d2b555434268b823b10988180a2c.webp)

● **Default Storage Path**: After installation, the system automatically creates a default folder named "**Voice Memos**" within the personal directory (e.g., `/home/username/Voice Memos`) to store recordings.

● **Add Folder**: Click the "**+**" button on the right to add a new NAS folder as a recording storage directory, enabling categorized management of recordings from different sources or types.

● **Scan Files**: Click the "**Scan**" button to re-index audio files in the current folder. If new recordings are added through other methods (such as the "**Files**" app), this feature synchronizes them with the Voice Memos list.

### General Settings

On the "**General**" settings page, you can customize conflict resolution policies for file uploads and view data processing notice for Voice Memos. When an uploaded recording has the same name as an existing file on the NAS, the system will handle it according to the selected settings.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-19/af685597fe824ad3834e08dd950a9a7b.webp)
