# UGOS Pro Desktop Guide

> **Article ID**: `61`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > UGOS Pro > UGOS Pro Desktop Guide`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/61  

---

**Applicable Models**: DH Series, DX Series, DXP Series, iDX Series

**Applicable Client**: UGREEN NAS PC Client, Web Browser

**Applicable Version**: UGOS Pro firmware 1.18.0.0076 and higher

The screenshots in this document are for reference only. The actual interface may vary slightly depending on the system or app version, please refer to the actual interface displayed.

After successfully logging in, you can access the UGOS Pro desktop. The system desktop provides an intuitive interface with commonly used features such as app management, device status, task notifications, and system settings. You can also perform operations such as restarting, shutting down, or logging out of your account.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-07/f77ac54d55594feda6b92d0ca357a9e8.webp)

## Desktop Main Menu

The main menu is located on the left panel of the desktop and provides quick access to and management of apps on the NAS device. You can:

● **Open installed apps**: Includes Docker container apps.

● **Install and update apps**: Find and open "**App Center**". In the app list, click "**Install**" or "**Update**" to complete the operation.

● **Manage desktop shortcuts**: After an app is installed, a desktop shortcut is automatically created. To delete a shortcut, find the corresponding app icon in the desktop main menu, right-click it, and select "**Delete shortcut**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-07/5e49e0a8de22479380791b8137c2a65d.webp)

## Top Taskbar

The taskbar is located at the top of the desktop. From left to right, the functions include Return to Desktop, My apps, Opened apps, Task Manager, Widget, LAN connection, Task Center, Notifications, Search, and Me, providing convenient and efficient access to system management features.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-07/32df495234544bbe8eb7da41de58ca2d.webp)

● **Return to Desktop:** Click to quickly return to the UGOS Pro desktop.

● **My apps**: View and open installed apps, Docker container apps, and other apps on the UGREEN NAS.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-07/2852548d46184a9ab080b1335d462451.webp)

● **Opened apps**: Displays currently running apps and services, allowing you to quickly switch between or close them.

● **Task Manager**: Displays real-time CPU and RAM usage, as well as upload task progress. Double-click to view more details, such as process usage, task progress, upload speed, and more.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-07/61610a535fe049a1926c58f5ab2c2a50.webp)

● **Widget**: View system information, resource monitoring, storage status, account activity, and more. Click "**Edit**" to add, delete, or rearrange widgets.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-07/42fcafe71784416091f275d2f0320511.webp)

● **LAN connection**: Displays the connection status of LAN, P2P, DDNS, and other connections.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-07/c07d3e51bd764debbc989d70933bb511.webp)

● **Task Center**: Records and manages all running and completed tasks. When performing file operations (such as file uploads or Volume migration), the Task Center opens a task window and displays the progress. Running tasks do not affect other operations, but may impact system performance under high load.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-07/f19da8720e354209aee684e6245a4854.webp)

● **Notifications**: Displays system messages, such as index rebuilding, error messages, status updates, and app installation notifications, helping you stay informed of system status.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-07/5dcaf2bf14014a10a1e7eb90dad96c37.webp)

● **Search**: Enter keywords to quickly search for documents, images, videos, audio files, apps, and other content.

● **Me**:Provides functions such as account settings, personalization settings, client version information, preference settings, system restart, shutdown, account switching, and logout.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-07/63a5e6465d6a4dfdb0660e9a297b0c80.webp)

## Me Settings

Through Me Settings, you can manage account activity, account security, Volume quota, and personalization settings.

### Local Account Settings

Click the "**Me**" icon in the upper-right corner of the desktop. In the expanded menu, click the username to open the account settings panel. You can perform the following operations:

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-07/c594380af2d4475080e0ecee9ca7ccfc.webp)

● **Change avatar**: Click the avatar to change it. You can upload an image from the NAS or local device, or use a default avatar. Supported formats include JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, and WEBP, with a maximum size of 8 MB.

● **Change password**: Set or change the account password and view the last password change time. The password must contain uppercase and lowercase letters. Numbers and symbols can also be used. The password length must be between 6 and 127 characters.

● **Change email address**: Change or bind the administrator email address to receive device-related information.

● **Enable two-factor authentication**: Enable or disable "**Two-factor authentication (2FA)**" for the account. After enabling this feature, you must enter both the account password and the second-step verification code (OTP) when logging in to improve account security.

● **View account activity**: Record and view activity logs for the current account, including account connection time, client name and IP address, and access protocol.

● **View volume capacity limit**: Displays the used capacity and capacity limit information of the Personal Folder.

### UGREEN Account Settings

Switch to the UGREEN Account Settings page to register a UGREEN account or log in directly.

● **View personal information**: Supports changing the UGREEN account nickname, viewing the registered country or region, and editing gender and birthday information.

● **Account security settings**: Supports changing the UGREEN account password, changing the bound phone number, unbinding devices, or deleting the account.

● **View my services and devices**: View the services currently enabled for the account and the bound NAS devices.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-07/3e41934bde414e4ea54ebeaa2d6469e0.webp)

## Personal Settings

Click the "**Me**" icon in the upper-right corner of the desktop. In the expanded menu, select "**Personal**" to open the personal settings panel. You can perform the following operations:

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-07/65dc5aae977e4bbc836e485ed1971d59.webp)

● **Wallpaper management**: Click "**Select wallpaper**" to change the wallpaper. You can upload an image from the NAS or local device, or use the default wallpaper. Wallpaper display modes include Fill, Fit, Stretch, Center, and Tile. Supported formats include BMP, JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, and GIF, with a maximum size of 8 MB.

● **Language & format settings**: Switch the language, adjust date and time formats, and select the temperature unit.

● **Menu bar**: Customize whether to show or hide items such as LAN connection status, Search, Widgets, and resource monitoring. The Widgets feature in the top taskbar can also be enabled or disabled from the Menu bar settings.
