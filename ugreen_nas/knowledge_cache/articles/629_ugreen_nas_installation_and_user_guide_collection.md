In this collection, you can find installation video tutorials for all UGREEN NAS product series.

Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced user, these tutorials will guide you through the installation, setup, and basic operations of UGREEN NAS.

For more detailed instructions, feel free to visit our Knowledge Center for additional tutorials.

# **Video Overview**

**Product Package Contents:** Learn about all the components included with your UGREEN NAS and their functions.

**3.5-Inch Hard Disk Installation:** Ensure proper installation of large-capacity hard disks.

**2.5-Inch Hard Disk Installation:** A guide for installing smaller hard disks, ideal for portable storage needs.

**M.2 Hard Disk Installation:** Quickly install M.2 hard disks to boost storage performance.

**Memory Replacement:** How to upgrade the NAS memory to meet higher performance requirements.

**Network Deployment:** Ensure your NAS device is stably connected to your network.

**Device Addition and Binding Settings:** How to add your UGREEN NAS to your network and complete the necessary binding process.
