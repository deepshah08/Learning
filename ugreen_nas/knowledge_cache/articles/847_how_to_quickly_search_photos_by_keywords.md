## Applicability

**Applicable Platforms**: UGREEN NAS PC Client, Web Browser, and UGREEN NAS mobile app.

**Applicable Versions**: UGOS Pro firmware version 1.15.0.0114 and later.

The descriptions in this document are for reference only. Actual interfaces and operation paths may vary slightly depending on system or application version updates. Please refer to the actual interface on your device.

## Feature Overview

After Photos completes intelligent recognition and categorization, you no longer need to manually browse through a large photo library. Simply enter keywords in the search bar, and the system will use underlying AI model data to quickly and accurately locate target photos.

## Prerequisites

Before using AI search, make sure the required AI models are enabled and that the Photos app has permission to access them.

### Configure and Authorize AI Models in Model Manager

1. Open the Model Manager app and go to "**Model Management**" from the left sidebar.

2. Locate the "**Image recognition**" and "**Text recognition**" models in the list, and ensure both are enabled.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/ea0240a6f95a4f2cb358dee1262f6b51.webp)

3. In the left sidebar of Model Manager, click the "**Photos**" app to enter the authorization page, then enable the switches for "**Image recognition**" and "**Text recognition**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/2b213065b1cd4f8791dc2d168f3bd188.webp)

### Enable Intelligent Recognition in Photos

1. Open the "**Photos**" app, go to "**Settings**", and click "**Intelligent**" settings.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/6cde373c2c5348f18a3d08e9133a0f1e.webp)

2. Check and confirm that both "**Image recognition**" (used for understanding image content and scene elements) and "**Text recognition**" (used for extracting text from photos) are enabled.

## Perform Keyword Search

1. Open the Photos app and click the search bar at the top of the page.

2. Enter the keyword you want to search for. The system currently supports the following common search scenarios:

● **Search for objects and scenes**: Enter the name of a specific object, animal, or environment (for example: "cat", "car").

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/026ad2aecc7c4d67a9aef1774041e648.webp)

● **Search text content (OCR)**: Enter text that actually appears in the image (e.g., tracking numbers in screenshots, text on documents, or road signs).

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/fb39fdb9edab4b4e8fc15c2322430235.webp)

3. After entering the keyword and pressing Enter, the system will immediately match results using image recognition and OCR data, then display all highly relevant photos in one place.
