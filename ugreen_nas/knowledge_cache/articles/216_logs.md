# Logs

> **Article ID**: `216`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Logs > Logs`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/216  

---

The **Logs** serves as an efficient tool for recording UGOS Pro system and application activities, monitoring, and managing operational status. It empowers you to track system and application behaviors for troubleshooting and performance optimization. Through the Logs, you can view and analyze various event logs to meet device operation and maintenance needs.

**Main Features**:

* **Event and Transmission Logging**: Comprehensively log UGOS Pro system and application operations, aiding in issue tracking and rapid root cause identification.
* **Archiving Functionality**: Automatically archive crucial logs to prevent loss, with customizable archiving strategies for flexible log storage management.
* **Automatic Notification**: Configure automatic notification policies to keep real-time tabs on system status, ensuring instant alerts for critical events and avoiding information oversights.

**Note**: The Logs is visible to administrators only, ensuring the security and privacy of log records. Regular log review and management by administrators contribute to maintaining system stability and security.
