# Why do photos appear differently in color on UGREEN NAS compared to the mobile photo album?

> **Article ID**: `716`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Photos > FAQ > Why do photos appear differently in color on UGREEN NAS compared to the mobile photo album?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `COMMON`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/716  

---

# **Issue Description**

When viewing photos using the UGREEN NAS mobile app, you may notice differences in color compared to viewing the same original photo in your phone’s native album—such as duller colors or darker brightness.

# **Cause Analysis**

To speed up image loading, the UGREEN NAS app displays thumbnail versions of photos by default. These thumbnails are compressed and downsampled, resulting in lower image quality and potential differences in color, contrast, and overall visual effect.

In contrast, your phone’s native album displays the original, uncompressed image, preserving full color and detail for a more accurate and vivid appearance.

# **Solution**

If you want to view photos on UGREEN NAS with the same visual quality as in your phone’s album, follow these steps:

1. Open the UGREEN NAS app and navigate to the target photo;
2. Tap the photo to enter the preview screen;
3. Tap the “View Original Photo” button — the system will load the uncompressed original image and display it in the best available quality.

# **Notes**

* Loading the original image takes longer than loading the thumbnail, so it’s recommended to view original photos over a Wi-Fi connection.
* Original images are usually large in size, and frequent loading may result in increased data usage.
* If the photo was taken with a high-resolution camera, it is recommended to enable original image viewing for the best visual experience.
