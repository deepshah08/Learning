# Hard Disk Installation Assistant (Hardware Disassembly and Risk Notification)

> **Article ID**: `137`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Storage > FAQ > Hard Disk Installation Assistant (Hardware Disassembly and Risk Notification)`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/137  

---

When installing hard disks into your UGREEN NAS, it is important to follow proper steps and be aware of the risks. Below is the hard disk installation guide along with associated risk notifications:

## **Mechanical Hard Disk Drive Installation Guide**

1. **Backup Data:** Before proceeding with the hard disk installation, make sure to fully backup any important data on your NAS.
2. **Check Compatibility:** Ensure that the hard disk you are installing is compatible with your UGREEN NAS model. You can consult the official hard disk compatibility list or user manual to confirm compatibility.
3. **Power Off the NAS:** Before installing the hard disk, power off your NAS and unplug the power cable.
4. **Remove the Hard Disk Tray:** Depending on your NAS model, open the hard disk slots. You may need to use a lock or other tools to remove the hard disk tray.
5. **Install the Hard Disk:** Insert the new hard disk into the hard disk tray, press the spring lock on the tray, and flip it to the back. Press the "press" area and pull out the arms, place the hard disk in the tray with the bottom facing down, align the screw holes, and push the arms back to secure it.
6. **Reinsert the Hard Disk Tray:** After securing the hard disk, slide the tray back into the main unit.
7. **Power On the NAS:** Plug the power cable back in and power on your device.
8. **Initialize the Hard Disk:** Once the NAS has booted up, go to the NAS "Storage" to initialize the newly installed hard disk. This can be done under [Storage] >[Storage].

## **Specific Hard Disk Installation Guide**

**For 2.5-inch hard disks:**

1. **Unlock the Tray:** Use the provided hard disk lock key and turn it to unlock.
2. **Remove the Tray:** Press the spring lock on the tray to remove it.
3. **Align the 2.5-inch Drive:** Flip the tray and align the screw holes of the 2.5-inch drive with the tray's holes.
4. **Secure the Drive:** Use screws to tighten the drive, then slide the tray back into place.
5. **Complete the Installation:** Once secure, press the handle to lock the tray, and the 2.5-inch drive is installed.

**Note:**

The removed fixation pillar can be placed in the first fixing hole for future installation of 3.5-inch drives.

**For 3.5-inch hard disks:**

1. **Unlock the Tray:** Use the provided hard disk lock key to unlock.
2. **Remove the Tray:** Press the spring lock to remove the tray.
3. **Insert the hard disk:** Flip the tray to the back, press the "press" area to open the arms, place the hard disk bottom facing down, align the screw holes, and secure the arms.
4. **Reinsert the Tray:** Slide the tray back into the main unit, and secure it.
5. **Complete the Installation:** Ensure the tray is tightly inserted and lock the tray using the handle.

**For M.2 hard disks**

**DXP2800 Model:**

1. **Power Off the Device:** Ensure the device is powered off.
2. **Unlock and Remove the Tray:** Unlock the tray and remove it. The M.2 slot is located on the left side inside the device.
3. **Insert the M.2 Drive:** Insert the M.2 drive into the slot and lock it in place with the clip.
4. **Reinsert the Tray:** After securing the drive, reinsert the tray and power on the device.
5. **Configure SSD Cache:** Once the device is stable, go to the UGREEN client to set up the SSD cache or storage.

**DXP4800 Model:**

1. **Power Off the Device:** Ensure the device is powered off.
2. **Remove Bottom Cover:** Use a screwdriver to remove the bottom cover, revealing M.2 slots and memory slots.
3. **Install the M.2 Drive:** Insert the M.2 drive into the slot and tighten the screws.
4. **Optional Cooling Paste:** Apply thermal paste to the M.2 drive if needed.
5. **Reassemble and Power On:** Reassemble the device and power it on. Configure SSD cache once the device is stable.

**Note**:

Before removing the M.2 hard disk, please go to the "UGREEN NAS" client to remove the SSD cache to prevent it from being used as a cache.

If you remove the hard disk without completing the cache removal process, data loss may occur.

The device comes with thermal silicone pads. Before installing the M.2 hard disk, make sure to peel off the protective film from the thermal silicone pad before installation.

## **Risk Notifications**

1. **Data Loss Risk:** Improper operation or issues with the hard disk could result in data loss. Always back up data before installation.
2. **Hard Disk Damage Risk:** Handle hard disks carefully to avoid damaging the drive or other NAS components.
3. **Electric Shock Risk:** Always power off and unplug the NAS before performing hard disk installation to avoid electric shock risks.
4. **Compatibility Risk:** Using incompatible hard disks may prevent the NAS from functioning correctly, or cause issues during operation.
5. **Warranty Risk:** Unauthorized disassembly may void the warranty. Always refer to the warranty terms before disassembling the device.

Before starting the installation, ensure that you have backed up all important data and carefully read the user manual or official guides. If you are unsure about any step, we recommend consulting UGREEN NAS support or community forums for additional guidance.
