## Introduction to the Reset Button

On UGREEN NAS devices, you can locate the "reset" button (usually found on the back of the device). Use a small object (such as a paperclip) to press and hold the button for 5 or 13 seconds until a beep sounds from the device, then release. The device will then perform the corresponding reset operation.

## Two Modes and Features of the Reset Button

In UGOS Pro, the reset button not only helps users reset the device but also offers various advanced functions. Depending on the duration the button is held, the device will execute different reset operations. Here is a detailed list of features:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Number** | **Feature Description** | **Reset Button Operation** |
| 1 | **Reset Administrator Password:** After a successful reset, you can log in using the admin username with a blank password (this account is only for password resetting). Once logged into the system, you can reset any administrator account, making it easier for administrators to log back in. | Press and hold for 5 seconds |
| 2 | **Reset Network Settings:** Restore all network settings to their default values, such as IP addresses. | Press and hold for 5 seconds |
| 3 | **Clear IP Block List:** Unblock all blocked IP addresses to allow access again. | Press and hold for 5 seconds |
| 4 | **Disable Firewall:** Turn off firewall settings to facilitate network connection troubleshooting. | Press and hold for 5 seconds |
| 5 | **Reset HTTP/HTTPS Ports to 9999/9443:** Restore HTTP port to 9999 and HTTPS port to 9443 to prevent access issues due to port conflicts. | Press and hold for 5 seconds |
| 6 | **Disable Auto-Startup for Virtual Machines and Docker:** Stop virtual machines and Docker from running automatically at system startup to save system resources. | Press and hold for 5 seconds |
| 7 | **Reset to factory settings****:** Reset the NAS device to its factory settings (does not delete data on the hard drive). | Press and hold for 5 seconds until you hear a beep, then continue holding for another 8 seconds until you hear a second beep before releasing. |
