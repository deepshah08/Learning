## Introduction

NFC (Near Field Communication) is a short-range wireless technology that enables data exchange between devices without physical contact, typically within 2 cm. With NFC, UGREEN NAS allows you to quickly connect your phone to the NAS with a simple tap, streamlining device registration and sign-in.

## Supported Models

The following UGREEN NAS models support NFC functionality: DH2300、DH2300 Plus、DH4300、DH4300 Plus.

Available models may vary by region. Please refer to the UGREEN official website for the models currently available in your region.

## Smartphone Operating System and Brand Compatibility

**Operating System Compatibility**

● **iOS**: Version 15 and above

● **Android**: Version 7 and above

**Android Brand Compatibility**

Major Android brands that are supported include, but are not limited to: Huawei, Honor, OPPO, Xiaomi, Vivo, Google, Samsung phones.

## Enable NFC on Your Phone

Using a Huawei phone as an example (the interface may vary by model; refer to your phone manufacturer’s instructions for details):

1. Open "**Settings**" on your phone.

2. Go to "**More connections**".

3. Find and tap "**NFC**".

4. Turn on NFC.

## Identify and Quickly Connect to the NAS via NFC

1. Hold an NFC-enabled phone near the NFC tag area on the NAS (the phone and NAS must be on the same LAN and within 2 cm of each other).

2. Tap the system notification that appears on your phone. You will be redirected to the download page or the UGREEN NAS app will open directly.

3. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete device registration or sign in, depending on the client’s current connection and usage status.

## Differences Across Mobile Operating Systems

### NFC Chip Location on Phones

On most phones, the NFC chip is located around the middle of the back of the device, usually near the rear camera or fingerprint sensor. You can look for the NFC symbol on the back of your phone or refer to the phone manufacturer’s documentation to determine the exact location.

### System Interaction Differences

Different phone brands and operating systems may respond differently when the phone is tapped against an NFC tag:

**Scenario 1: UGREEN NAS app not installed**

● Android: The behavior varies by phone manufacturer. The phone may open the app store directly, display download options for you to choose from, or show a system notification prompting you to select a download method and then redirect you to the corresponding page.

● iOS: When the phone is held near the NFC tag, a system notification appears. Tap the notification to go directly to the App Store download page.

**Scenario 2: UGREEN NAS app installed (not open)**

● Android: Some brands open the app directly and display the NFC pop-up, while others first show a system notification. Tap the notification to open the app and display the NFC pop-up.

● iOS: When the phone is held near the NFC tag, a system notification appears. Tap the notification to go to the app page, then tap Open to return to the app and display the NFC pop-up. When the phone is held near the NFC tag, a system notification appears. Tap the notification to go to the app page, then tap Open to return to the app and display the NFC pop-up.

**Scenario 3: UGREEN NAS app installed (open)**

● Android: Some brands display the NFC pop-up directly, while others first show a system notification. Tap the notification to display the NFC pop-up.

● iOS: When the phone is held near the NFC tag, a system notification appears. Tap the notification to go to the app page, then tap Open to return to the app and display the NFC pop-up.
