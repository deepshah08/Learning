The descriptions in this document are for reference only. The actual interface may vary depending on system or app updates, please refer to the actual interface.

## Introduction

The Music app lets users manage and play music files stored on the NAS. Users can create **Personal library** and **Shared library**. After music files are added to a music library, the system scans the audio files and reads embedded metadata, such as ID3 tags, to automatically organize and categorize the music.

The Music app supports browsing music by:

● Albums

● Artists

● Genres

● Folders

Music can be played on:

● Mobile app

● Web browser

● UGREEN NAS PC client

The Music app also supports album artwork and local lyrics, as well as playback of popular lossless audio formats such as FLAC, APE, and WAV.

## Installation and Access

1. Open "**App Center**", find the "**Music**" app, and click "**Install**".

2. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation.

3. installation, click the Music icon on the desktop or in All Apps to start using the app.

## Create a Library

Before using the Music app, you need to create a library and link the folder where your music files are stored. Once linked, the system scans the audio files in the folder and identifies their music information.

There are two types of libraries:

● **Personal library**

● **Shared library**

It is recommended to organize your music files into dedicated folders by artist, album, or genre before adding them to a music library.

## Create a Personal Library

A Personal music library is used to manage the current user's own music files.

1. Open the "**Music**" app and click "**Settings**" at the top of the page.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/f4c7def775df42a591a3d1e30ca42317.webp)

2. On the Settings page, select Personal library, click "**Add**", select the folder containing the music files, and confirm.

3. After the folder is added, the system starts scanning the folder linked to the library.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/13c8259e8f5e462dba194010d5a9fc93.webp)

## Create a Shared Library

A Shared library is suitable for multiple users to access and play music.

1. Open the "**Music**" app and click "**Settings**" at the top of the page.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/02006f760f6f48d8a737b0fe9a8b0934.webp)

2. On the Settings page, select Shared library, click "**Add**", select the folder containing the music files, and confirm.

3. Scroll down and configure the music information, additional settings, and user access permissions as needed.

4. After the folder is added, the system starts scanning the linked folder.

## Browse Music

After uploading music files and completing the library scan, you can browse your music in the Music app. Use the left sidebar to browse music by category. On the right side of the library, you can use "**Switch View**" and "**Sort**". The following views are available:

● **List**

● **Grid**

**Note**: When songs are displayed in "**Grid**" view, album artwork is supported only for music files in `ape`, `wav`, and `flac` formats.

2. Hover over the song and click "**···**" > "**Music information**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/129832f2d69a49ce8c9be576a329eba5.webp)

3. On the Music information page, edit the information as needed, then click "**Save**".

## Sidebar Overview

The left sidebar of the Music app provides quick access to different music categories.

● **Library**: Displays music files in the created music libraries.

● **Folders**: Browse music by the folders where the music files are stored.

● **Albums**: Displays music by album.

● **Genres**: Displays music by genre.

● **Artists**: Displays music by artist.

● **Recently Played**: Displays recently played music.

● **My Favorites**: Displays favorited music.

● **Playlist**: Displays playlists created by the user.

## Create and Edit Playlists

Users can create playlists to organize frequently played songs.

**Create a Playlist**:

1. Open the "**Music**" app and find "**Playlist**" in the left sidebar.

2. Click "**+**" next to Playlist, enter a playlist name, and press **Enter** to create it.

After the playlist is created, you can add songs to it.

**Note**: Playlists cannot be shared with other users.

**Edit Playlist Information**:

1. Click the playlist name in the left sidebar to open the playlist details page.

2. Click the "**Edit**" icon next to the playlist name.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/1561b32632214304885226f5c2fa0c16.webp)

3. Edit the playlist cover, name, and description as needed.

4. Click "**Save**".

After saving, the playlist information is updated.

## Sort Songs in a Playlist

Users can sort songs in a playlist based on their preferences. By default, songs are sorted by added time in descending order.  
Each playlist has its own independent sorting rule.

The following sorting options are supported:

● **Manual sorting**: Press and hold a song, then drag it to the desired position.

● **By added time**: The default sorting method.

● **By name**: Sorts songs alphabetically or by Chinese character order.

● **By artist**: Sorts songs by artist name alphabetically or by Chinese character order.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/647be1776b104fc49e0150a2bdc52d77.webp)

When using Manual sorting, you can reorder up to **2,000** songs at a time. After adjusting the order, click "**Confirm**" to apply the changes. Manual sorting results are synced across devices.

## Music Metadata Recognition

The music library reads extended attributes from music files, such as ID3 tags, to identify music information. The system mainly uses metadata embedded in audio files to categorize and organize music, rather than identifying music by file name.

Recognized metadata includes:

● Title

● Artist

● Album

● Year

● Genre

● Album artwork

Whether local lyrics and album artwork are displayed correctly depends on the embedded file information, local lyrics files, and format support on the current device. You can use a music management tool, such as **Music Tag**, to complete or edit metadata tags. For details, refer to the "[Music Tag Web Quick Start Guide](https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/detail/article/en-US/672?id=4383&type=tag002&clientType=PC&articleVersion=1.0) ".

## Supported Lyrics Format

The Music app supports standard **LRC** lyrics files with synchronized lyrics playback. The lyrics file must meet the following requirements:

● The lyrics file is in .lrc format

● The lyrics file has the same file name as the audio file

● The lyrics file is stored in the same folder as the audio file

Once these requirements are met, the player can automatically read and display the lyrics.

## Supported Audio Formats

**PC / Web browser**: `.aac`, `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.ogg`, `.flac`, `.wma`, `.dff`, `.dsf`, `.alac`, `.aiff`, `.ape`, `.m4a`.

**Mobile (iOS/Android)**: `.aac`, `.aif`, `.aiff`, `.ape`, `.au`, `.flac`, `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.ra`, `.dts`, `.wma`, `.ac3`, `.amr`, `.m4r`, `.mka`, `.alac`, `.m4a`, `.ogg`.

Audio format support may vary by client. If an audio file cannot be played, first check whether the current client supports that format.

## Share a Music Library with Family Members

Administrators can create separate user accounts for family members and assign music library access permissions to those accounts. Once configured, family members can sign in to the UGREEN NAS client with their own accounts and open the Music app to access authorized library content.

1. Open "**Control Panel**", then click "**User Management**" > "**Add**" to create a user account for the family member.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/743b7e74d429480aaf310160d0e45990.webp)

2. Open the "**Music**" app and click "**Settings**" at the top.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/232a19bf401f4b50af5751270c651e52.webp)

3. Click "**Shared library**", find the newly created user under Permission management, and set the appropriate library access permission.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/cd0bc876faf946e3bf7097c85ec26c1e.webp)

After the settings are applied, ask the family member to open the UGREEN NAS client and sign in with the newly created user account. After signing in, open the "**Library**" to access the authorized library content.

**Note**: If the family member cannot see the shared library after signing in, check whether the user's library permission is set to "**Access denied**".
