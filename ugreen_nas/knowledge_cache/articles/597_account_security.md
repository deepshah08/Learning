# Account Security

> **Article ID**: `597`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Control Panel > Security > Account Security`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/597  

---

In the "**Control Panel">"Security"**>"**Account security**" settings page, you can enhance account security by managing the two-factor authentication, account activity and account blocking features.

● Two-factor authentication (2FA) provides an extra layer of security for UGOS Pro user accounts. Once enabled, users must enter both their account password and a dynamic verification code when logging in, preventing unauthorized access.

● Account activity lets you view currently connected devices and the real-time status of your account.

● Account blocking helps prevent abnormal login behavior by setting automatic lock conditions, and allows you to add trusted accounts to a whitelist to further enhance security.

## Two-factor Authentication (2FA)

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) is a security feature designed to enhance account protection. Once enabled, in addition to entering your password, you must also enter a one-time password (OTP) to complete the login process. This effectively prevents unauthorized access—even if someone obtains your username and password, they would still need the OTP generated by your mobile app to log in.

Enabling 2FA increases the security of your account and system, helping to prevent unauthorized access.

The one-time password is generated through a mobile app that supports the OTP protocol, such as Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator.

For detailed instructions, please refer to [**2FA Two-Factor Authentication**](https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/eyJjb2RlIjoiMiYmNTEwIn0=) **.**

## Account Activity

On the "**Account activity**" page, you can view currently connected devices and the real-time status of your account.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-12-22/b56ebe735c87467d8696ffc74e4255c9.webp)

### Viewing and Filtering Account Activity

The system records all account login details, including the most recent connection time, client, source IP address, and accessed service protocol.

You can quickly locate specific activity records by **"User Type"** or **"Username"**.

### IP Address Display Rules

Depending on your login method, the system will display different source IP addresses in account activity:

> Note: Currently, the system does not support recording source IPs for devices accessing the NAS via NAT traversal or reverse proxy.

● **DDNS Login:** The system shows the NAS's public IPv4 from the router or its IPv6 address.

● **NAT Traversal:** The system shows the NAS's local network IP address.

● **UGREENlink:** The source IP is displayed as `127.0.0.1` (localhost).

● **DDNS + Reverse Proxy:** The system shows the NAS's local network IP address.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-12-22/5531975c00ec46eeb27127a45606da59.webp)

### Account Activity Record Rules

Please note the following rules regarding activity record storage:

● **Cleared on Reboot:** All account activity records are cleared when the NAS restarts. Recording resumes from the moment the device finishes rebooting.

● **Only the Latest State is Retained:** If the same device logs in multiple times, the system only keeps the most recent activity information. Historical login records are overwritten and not listed repeatedly.

> For example, if you log in five times using different methods after a reboot, only the most recent login method and IP will appear in the list.

### Terminating Device Logins

If you notice suspicious connections or want to disconnect a device, locate the target device in the list and click **"Terminate"**.

The terminated device will be forcibly logged out. When it attempts to access the NAS again, the user must re-enter the account password to verify.

## Account Blocking and Whitelist Management

To enhance system security, you can configure "**Account blocking**" policies to prevent malicious activities such as brute-force password attacks. At the same time, you can use a "**whitelist"** to ensure that trusted accounts are not affected by blocking rules.

## Configure Account Blocking

1. Go to "**Security**">"**Account security**", locate "**Account blocking**", turn on the switch, and click "**Edit**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-12-22/be9e49b842c34b378df30b9ab0d4e3d0.webp)

2. Configure the rules as needed. You can adjust the threshold that triggers a block (default: 6 failed login attempts within 5 minutes) and set the block duration (default: 5 minutes). Once the duration expires, the system will automatically unblock the account.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-12-22/b9328ac697554b688c39f9970eddc752.webp)

3. Click "**OK**" to apply the settings.

### Configure the Whitelist

You can add trusted local users or domain users to the whitelist. Accounts on the whitelist are exempt from automatic blocking rules and will not be blocked even after multiple failed login attempts.

1. On the "**Account blocking**" settings page, switch to the "**Whitelist**"tab.

2. Click "**Add**", then choose "**Add user**" or "**Add domain user**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-12-22/065710f8ef28470d93d497f58ababe09.webp)

3. From the list of available users, select the accounts you trust.

4. Click "**Confirm**" to complete the addition.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-12-22/8254e3df764941d0977971843d8a95c1.webp)

### Managing the Blocklist and Whitelist

After the configuration is complete, you can view, unblock, or remove users at any time.

● On the "**Blocklist**" and "**Whitelist**" pages, you can view currently blocked IP addresses/users as well as trusted accounts that have been added to the whitelist.

● In the "**Blocklist**", you can manually remove users who were blocked by mistake, which immediately unblocks them.

● The "**Whitelist**" supports selecting and removing multiple users at once, improving management efficiency.
