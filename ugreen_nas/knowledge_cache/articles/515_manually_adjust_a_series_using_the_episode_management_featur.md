After adding a library and scanning media files in the "Theater", you may encounter the following issues:

● Some episodes are not fully recognized, resulting in missing episodes or incorrect order.

● Behind-the-scenes footage or variety show clips are not recognized as part of the series but appear as independent videos or unrecognized items.

With the "Episode Management" feature, you can manually correct recognition results and adjust the episode order. For example, you can merge behind-the-scenes clips into the target series and set them to play as the final episode.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/ea8ef28e090d4b86a363d691d6a011a3.webp)

## Add Videos to Episodes

When behind-the-scenes or unrecognized videos are not detected as part of episodes, you can manually add them to a specific series.

**Steps:**

1. Locate the unrecognized or incorrectly recognized video file in the "Theater"

2. Hover over the video poster, click the "···" button, and select "Add to episodes".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/53e7ede15e494851b0f5f534f4831db1.webp)

3. In the pop-up window, select the episodes the video belongs to and click "Comfirm". The system will add the video to the designated series.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/7c4685657b8340bfbaa124c5732b2073.webp)

## Adjust Episode Order

If the episode order is incorrect (e.g., Episode 7 is recognized as Episode 6), you can adjust it as follows:

1. Select the episode you want to adjust and open its details page in the "Theater".

2. Click "···">"Episode management".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/43c34eeffbb742478602d8025dca3d6b.webp)

3. Modify the episode number in the management interface. Changes will take effect immediately.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/4d99cdd6c79d4ead874d61c600cc266a.webp)

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: Do I need to rescan the media library after adjusting the episode order?

**A:** No. Changes take effect immediately without rescanning.
