# UGREEN NAS UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) Compatibility List

> **Article ID**: `136`  
> **Category**: `Troubleshooting > Product Compatibility List > UGREEN NAS UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) Compatibility List`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/136  

---

To ensure system stability and optimal performance, it is recommended to prioritize **UPS** devices that have passed UGREEN's official compatibility testing when making a purchase.

For detailed accessory compatibility across different UGREEN NAS series, please refer to the [**UGREEN NAS Product Compatibility List**](https://nas.ugreen.com/pages/compatibility) , where you can find the latest supported models and compatibility details.

**Note**: To use **UGREEN official UPS devices** with **DH2600 and DX4600 series NAS**, please make sure the system software has been upgradedto **UGOS Pro**.

This system version requirement does not apply to non-UGREEN UPS devices.
