# Why Can't Photos Be Added to an Recognized Person

> **Article ID**: `846`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Photos > FAQ > Why Can't Photos Be Added to an Recognized Person`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/846  

---

## Applicability

**Applicable platforms**: UGREEN NAS PC client and web browser.

**Applicable versions**: UGOS Pro firmware 1.15.0.0114 and later.

The screenshots and interfaces in this document are for reference only. Actual displays may vary depending on system or application versions. Some features may differ across versions; please refer to your actual interface for accuracy.

## Issue Description

In the "**Categorization**" section of Photos, multiple faces have already been automatically recognized by the system. However, when specific photos are selected in the "**Library**" and "**Add to People**" is chosen, those photos cannot be added to the already identified person groups.

## Analysis

In Photos, unnamed face groups are treated only as temporary system clustering data and cannot be used as targets for manually adding photos. A specific "**name**" must first be assigned to the person group before it can be activated as a manually manageable person profile.

## Solution

Please follow the steps below, firstly assign a name to the person, then perform the add operation.

**Step 1: Assign a Name to the Recognized Person**

1. In the left sidebar of Photos, click "**Categorization**" and select "**People**."

2. In the list of recognized faces, locate the target person to whom photos should be added.

3. Right-click the person's avatar and select "**Rename**" from the pop-up menu.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/5b064ec7dae24d118bd3d35966a10e22.webp)

4. Enter a specific name for the person (e.g., "Peter") and confirm to save it.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/a2022f774a2e4340ae4277b3fd38a4e1.webp)

**Step 2: Add Photos to the Person**

1. After naming is completed, return to the "**Library**" page of Photos and select one or multiple photos to be added.

2. After selection, a floating action bar will appear at the bottom of the page. Click the "**...**" button.

3. In the pop-up menu, select the "**Add to People**" option.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/20051e615b1f4d06a7acd987ca792161.webp)

4. The person that was just named will now appear in the selectable list. Click the person's name to successfully complete the addition.
