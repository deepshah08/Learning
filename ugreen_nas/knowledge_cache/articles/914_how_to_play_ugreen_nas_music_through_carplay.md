# How to Play UGREEN NAS Music Through CarPlay

> **Article ID**: `914`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Music > How to Play UGREEN NAS Music Through CarPlay`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `MOBILE`

## Applicability

**Applicable Client**: UGREEN NAS Mobile App (iOS).

**Applicable Version**: NAS firmware 1.18.0.0032 or later.

Screenshots are for reference only. The actual interface may vary depending on the system or app version, please refer to the actual display.

## Introduction

This document explains how to connect your car infotainment system and play music from UGREEN NAS through CarPlay.

The page names may vary slightly depending on the phone OS version and car infotainment system model. Please refer to the actual pages displayed on your device. If you cannot complete the setup, contact your phone manufacturer's customer support for assistance.

## Connect iPhone to CarPlay

Make sure your car infotainment system supports CarPlay. Connect your iPhone to the car infotainment system using a USB cable, or follow the car system instructions to connect to wireless CarPlay.

When prompted on your iPhone, follow the instructions to allow the connection. The connection is successful when the CarPlay interface appears on the car display.

## Enable Background App Refresh for UGREEN NAS

After connecting to the in-vehicle system, enable Background App Refresh on your iPhone to ensure uninterrupted playback of UGREEN NAS music. Allow UGREEN NAS to run in the background.

1. Open "**Settings**" on your iPhone and tap "**General**".

2. Tap "**Background App Refresh**", find "**UGREEN NAS**" in the App list, and turn on the switch.

## Play UGREEN NAS Music Through the In-Vehicle System

After completing the above settings, keep your iPhone connected to the in-vehicle system.

1. Open the UGREEN NAS App on your phone and go to the Music App.

2. Select a song and start playback, the music will play through the in-vehicle system.

## Notes

● Make sure the UGREEN NAS App and device firmware are updated to the latest versions.

● If playback is interrupted, check whether background activity for UGREEN NAS is restricted on your phone.

● If the in-vehicle system cannot recognize your phone, try using a different USB cable or reconnecting wireless CarPlay.

● The settings paths may vary by phone brand. The steps in this document are for reference only, please refer to the actual interface displayed on your device.

● If you still cannot complete the setup, contact your phone manufacturer or in-vehicle manufacturer for assistance.
