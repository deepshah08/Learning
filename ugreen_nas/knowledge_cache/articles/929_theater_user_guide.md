## Applicability

**Applicable Clients:** UGREEN NAS PC Client (Windows/macOS), Web Browser

**Applicable Version:** UGOS Pro Firmware 1.18.0.0093 or later

The descriptions in this document are for reference only. The interface and operation paths may vary depending on system or app updates. Please refer to the actual interface.

## App Overview

The Theater app lets you centrally manage and play videos stored on your NAS. After you create a media library, the system automatically scans the video files in the library and intelligently identifies film information based on file names. Once identified, it automatically matches details such as film posters, descriptions, cast, and crew.

You can watch films in Theater on the UGREEN NAS PC, mobile, and TV clients, with viewing data synced across devices.

## Install and Access

1. Open "**App Center**" and find the "**Theater**" app.

2. Click "**Install**", then follow the on-screen instructions to complete the setup.

3. After installation, click the Theater icon on the desktop or in All Apps to get started.

## Create Library

Before using Theater, you need to create a media library and link the folder containing your videos. After the folder is linked, the system scans the video files in the folder and intelligently identifies film information based on the file names.

1. Open the "**Theater**" app and click "**Settings**" at the top.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-10/bc1d19e322724bd8939db6e7f49dec83.webp)

2. On the Settings page, click "**Library**" > "**New library**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-10/7a62d2ebd5ea45b18450ae66f0cd2ee4.webp)

3. Set the library name and language, then select the folder containing your videos.

4. Scroll down and configure Video Information, Additional Configuration, and user access permissions for the library as needed.

5. When finished, click "**Apply**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-10/39681837e55f42b2b809565380ac2e99.webp)

After the media library is created, the system starts scanning the linked folder. Once the scan is complete, Theater intelligently identifies and matches the following film information based on the video file names:

● Film posters

● Film descriptions

● Cast and crew information

● Film categories

● Video file information

If new videos are added to the folder, rescan the media library so Theater can identify the newly added content.

**Note:** Film identification results depend on file naming, folder structure, and data source settings. Use standardized film titles when naming video files.

## Browse and Play

### Homepage

After opening Theater, a carousel appears at the top of the Homepage and can display up to five items. Scroll down to view more content sections.

You can customize which sections appear on the Homepage and their display order in "**Settings**" > "**Homepage**". The "**Search**" and "**Settings**" options are located at the top of the page.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-10/79efdcf1635f469b917515b1a8acda35.webp)

### View Film Details

Click a film poster to open the film details page. The page typically displays the following information:

● Landscape poster

● Film duration

● Watch progress

● Film description

● Cast and crew information

● Video file information

You can view film details on this page and edit the film information using the available editing options.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-10/83c20ae94f8f4756bd19c494e273ac8e.webp)

### Play Videos

The Theater player supports multiple video formats. During playback, the following options are available:

● Video quality

● Subtitle selection

● Audio language selection

● Picture adjustment

● Subtitle download

● External subtitles

For HDR and Dolby Vision content, the player applies color optimization to improve the viewing experience.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-10/c341317dc6004f7b85bcfb279c044dce.webp)

## Supported Video Formats

The Theater app supports a wide range of common video formats. You can directly play and manage the following media files and folders:

● Video file formats: 3gp, asf, avi, m2ts, m4v, mkv, mov, mp4, mpeg, mpg, rm, rmvb, ts, vob, wmv, and more

● Disc images and folders: ISO files and BDMV folders

## Share Library with Family Members

Administrators can create separate user accounts for family members and configure their access permissions to Theater media libraries. Once set up, family members can sign in to the UGREEN NAS client with their own accounts, open Theater, and watch content in the media libraries they are authorized to access.

1. Open "**Control Panel**", then click "**User Management**">"**Add**" to create a user account for the family member.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-10/197e168ba5194055a6b1740c238e376a.webp)

2. Open the "**Theater**" app and click "**Settings**" at the top.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-10/e6c98c821eba40dba88ace7e1bc8c6b9.webp)

3. Click "**User permission**", find the newly created user, and click "**Set permissions**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-10/87f06d0fa02740b6ab3f1612f7e4ff57.webp)

4. Set the libraries you want to share to "**Allow access**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-10/8892f9c34d3345fb953ce1f9975ccd5f.webp)

5. Click "**Apply**" to save the settings.

Once everything is set up, your family member can sign in to the UGREEN NAS client with their new account, open "**Theater**", and start watching content from the media libraries they have access to.

**Note**: If the family member cannot see the corresponding media library after signing in, check whether **Allow access** has been enabled for that user's library permissions.
