# How to check the IP Address of Your UGREEN NAS?

> **Article ID**: `617`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Control Panel > FAQ > How to check the IP Address of Your UGREEN NAS?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/617  

---

You can check the IP address of your UGREEN NAS device using the following methods:

#### **1. Check via Control Panel**

* Go to [Control Panel] and navigate to [Network] > [Network Connection].

* On the [Network Connection] page, you can find the current IP address assigned to your NAS device (usually listed under LAN1 or LAN2).

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250317/ee44acb8-a9d0-4aef-93ed-9f0c8b2815bd.png)

#### **2.** **Check via Router**

* Open a browser and enter your router's management address (typically `192.168.1.1` or `192.168.0.1`) to access the router's settings.
* In the router's interface, look for" Connected Devices" or "Device Management" (options may vary by router brand).
* Find your UGREEN NAS device in the list, which can usually be identified by its device name or MAC address.
* The device information will display the IP address assigned to your NAS.
