# Build Your Own Private Cinema with Theater

> **Article ID**: `432`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Theater > FAQ > Build Your Own Private Cinema with Theater`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/432  

---

Theater is a powerful video management and playback application provided by UGREEN NAS. It helps users easily build a home theater system, manage their personal movie library, and enjoy a cinematic viewing experience across multiple devices.

## **What Can Theater Do?**

* **Smart Metadata Scraping**: Automatically recognizes movie titles, release years, cast, plot summaries, posters, and more once videos are added.
* **Smooth Multi-Device Playback**: Watch on phones, tablets, computers, or TVs with synchronized viewing progress. Start a movie on your home computer and continue on your phone while on the go.
* **Poster Wall Display**: Enjoy an immersive visual experience with full-screen poster slideshows and a categorized layout similar to popular streaming platforms.
* **High-Quality Video Support**: Compatible with premium video formats such as Dolby Vision, HDR, Blu-ray, and 4K sources.
* **Flexible Library Management**: Create multiple libraries with customizable user permissions—ideal for privacy protection and family sharing.

## **Preparation**

Before creating a library, make sure to upload your personal video resources to your UGREEN NAS. We recommend naming files in a standardized format (e.g., `SeriesName.2022.S01E01`) to improve metadata matching accuracy. If a video is not recognized correctly, hover over the cover image, click the three dots in the lower right corner, and select Manual identification to re-enter the correct title for identification.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250723/25cd4d86-ec17-499b-bfd7-d383202d1f1d.png)

* **Method 1: Local Upload**

Drag and drop your video files or use the upload button to import them to the NAS.

It’s recommended to organize your files into folders such as Movies, TV Shows, Animation, etc., to make it easier to create corresponding libraries later.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250723/58bef797-2414-4ed3-b10d-fabb55662195.png)

* **Method 2: Cloud Drive Download**

Use the built-in cloud drive tools in the NAS to download video resources directly to the NAS. Supported platforms include Baidu Netdisk, 115, OneDrive, Alibaba Cloud Drive, Quark Cloud, and more.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250723/3b4f015a-6f03-4895-b3e1-989fc204517c.png)

* **Method 3: BT/PT and Other Online Downloads**

Use built-in download applications (such as Xunlei, Downloads, qBittorrent, etc.) to directly download video resources to the NAS via BT, PT, or magnet links — no need to download to your computer first and then upload.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250723/2dbef412-89a2-4179-a375-36e490ec9e1e.png)

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250723/71f9ee15-e145-4d92-8893-3397b4a3ef13.png)

## **Create User Accounts (Optional)**

If you want family members to access video resources, you can create accounts for them in advance:

1. Go to [Control Panel] > [User Management] > [Add] > [Create];
2. Set the username, password, and role (Administrator/Regular User);
3. Later, you can assign access permissions to the libraries for each user.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250723/d229c093-fb15-4e3c-9baf-c499612d3086.png)

## **Create Library & Assign Permissions**

1. Open [Theater] and click the "Settings" button in the top right corner;

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250723/56a4f0e9-b958-4dbf-873b-7423873e9275.png)

2. Go to [Library] > click [+ New Library];

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250723/8136c23a-eaf5-4a1d-9a3e-57cc30896868.png)

3. Set library parameters:
4. Enter the library name and select the language.
5. **Check TMDB:** When enabled, the system will use [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/)’s movie database to automatically fetch posters, plot summaries, cast information, and other metadata.

* **Check Intelligent Recognition:** The system will use built-in scraping functions to analyze and match local video resources, retrieving posters and detailed cast and crew information.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250723/640abb01-3000-497a-a84a-483bae92d2de.png)

6. **Check Read Local Information First:** When enabled, the system will read the movie’s folder and prioritize any existing poster files as the cover image.
7. **Check Automatically Add to Collection:** If your video resources include TV series or movie series, they will be automatically identified and grouped into the same collection.
8. **Check Filter Files Smaller Than [customizable] MB:** This prevents everyday short videos (such as phone recordings) from being scanned and recognized.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250723/2bb0c8a8-4f12-46a4-bfeb-4d845e1172db.png)

9. **Permission Settings** (decide who can see this library and with whom you want to share the video resources):
10. **Access Allowed**: The user can see this library in their Theater after logging in and can play the videos normally.
11. **Access Denied**: The user cannot see this library at all and cannot find related content via search.
12. **Default**: Administrators are allowed access by default, while regular users are denied access by default.

After clicking "Apply", the system will automatically generate a poster wall (generation time depends on the number of files).

## **Enjoy a Rich Viewing Experience**

* Once the library posters are generated, the homepage will display a poster slideshow.

* Library types can be browsed by category;
