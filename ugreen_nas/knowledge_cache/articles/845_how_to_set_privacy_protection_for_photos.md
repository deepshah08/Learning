# How to Set Privacy Protection for Photos

> **Article ID**: `845`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Photos > How to Set Privacy Protection for Photos`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/845  

---

> **Applicable Platform**: UGREEN NAS PC client and web browser.
>
> **Applicable Version**: UGOS Pro 1.15.0.0114 and above
>
> Screenshots and interfaces in this document are for reference only. Actual displays may vary depending on system or application version. Some features may change across versions, please refer to your actual interface.

The **Set Privacy Protection** feature helps hide private photos, preventing them from being directly displayed in the "Library".

## Apply Privacy Protection to a Single Photo

1. On the "**Library**" page, click any photo to open the detail view, then click the "**...**" icon in the top-right corner.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-30/1a05f4ca7c4b4e9097d532c8f897b235.webp)

2. Select "**Set privacy protection**" to mark the photo as sensitive.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-30/8bb039849c2e4187bf327b8a5d3033cf.webp)

3. Protected photos will appear blurred on the "**Library**" homepage.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-30/def2812246924537be38d771ee47dc27.webp)

## Apply Privacy Protection in Bulk

1. On the "**Library**" page, select multiple photos.

2. Click the "**...**" button in the bottom action bar and choose "**Set privacy protection**" to mark them in bulk.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-30/16337b6e970e43f5a1eaa876ef9a2bc0.webp)

**Note**: To remove privacy protection, right-click or select the protected photos and choose "**Remove privacy protection**" from the menu.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-30/01136288c1a4449eab479556c1601470.webp)

## Automatic Detection of Sensitive Photos

The privacy protection feature requires the administrator to enable "**Sensitive content recoginition**" in "**Intelligent**" of "**Settings**" before it can be used.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-30/20c92cdecdca47a4825b8fb42f063cf0.webp)
