# How to Quickly Manage Friend Uploads and Shared Links in the Photos App?

> **Article ID**: `368`

**Applicable models**: DH series, DX series, DXP series, and iDX series

**Applicable clients**: UGREEN NAS PC client and web browser

**Applicable version**: UGOS Pro firmware 1.15.12.0051 and later

## Scenario Description

After you share an album or photo with others through "**Internal sharing**" or "**External link sharing**", or create an "**Invite friends to upload**" link, you may need to manage the links you have created. For example, you may want to check which links are still active, extend the validity period, change the access password, or stop sharing.

With "**Sharing management**" and "**Friend upload management**" in the "**Photos**" App, you can view, edit, and delete all sharing links in one place for easier management.

## Steps

### Manage Sharing Links

"**Sharing management**" is used to manage all created external sharing links, including album sharing links and links for sharing one or more photos.

1. Open the "**Photos**" app and click the "**Settings**" icon at the bottom of the sidebar.

2. Select "**Sharing management**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-25/7b6a025dadc3466795a05d2befa5a34f.webp)

3. After entering the "**Sharing management**" page, you can perform the following actions:

● **Clear invalid links:** Click "**Clear invalid links**" to remove all expired links at once.

● **Sort links:** Sort the link list by "**Sharing time**", "**Validity**", or "**Type of sharing**".

● **Manage External link sharing:** Click the "**…**" icon on the right of any link to edit sharing information, such as validity, password, and permissions; copy the link again and send it to others; or stop sharing. After the link is deleted, others will no longer be able to access it.

● **Manage Internal sharing:** Add users in batches, set album access permissions, or stop Internal sharing.

### Manage Friend Upload Links

"**Friend upload management**" is used specifically to manage the "**Invite friends to upload**" links you have created. These links allow non-NAS users to upload photos to a specified album through a link.

1. On the "**Settings**" page, select "**Friend upload management**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-25/586716291adb48f683d9960ae5c6cf14.webp)

2. After entering the Friend upload management page, you can perform the following actions:

● **Clear invalid links:** Click "**Clear invalid links**" to delete all expired upload links in batches.

● **Sort links:** Sort the link list by "**Sharing time**" or "**Validity**".

● **Edit link:** You can copy the link address. Click the "**…**" icon on the right of the link to modify the link name, note, validity, storage location, and password, or delete the invitation link. Once deleted, the created link will become invalid immediately.

## Additional Notes

● After a link expires, the system automatically marks it as "Invalid", but does not delete it immediately, so you can still view the history. You can manually clear invalid links.

● When creating an "Invite friends to upload" invitation link, you can specify which folder the photos are saved to. By default, photos are saved to the default folder associated with the album. You can also change the storage location when editing the link on the management page.
