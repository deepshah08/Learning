## Introduction

UGREEN NAS App Center now provides third-party apps, allowing users to install and update apps through a unified platform. Third-party app updates are based on the versions available in App Center. Once a new version has been adapted and released in App Center, you can update the app.

## How to Update Third-Party Apps?

1. Open "**App Center**" and find the third-party app that needs to be updated.

2. If a new version is available, an "**Update**" button will be displayed on the app page.

3. Click "**Update**" and wait for the update to complete.

After the update is complete, you can use the latest version of the app.

## Why Can't App Center Update to the Latest Version Even Though It Has Been Released on the Third-Party Official Website?

Third-party apps on UGREEN NAS are adapted and distributed through App Center. After a new version is released on the third-party official website, it usually needs to go through adaptation and compatibility testing to ensure stable operation on UGOS Pro.

Therefore, there may be a difference between the release time of the version on the third-party official website and the version available in App Center. If the "**Update**" button is not displayed in App Center, it means the new version has not yet completed adaptation or release.
