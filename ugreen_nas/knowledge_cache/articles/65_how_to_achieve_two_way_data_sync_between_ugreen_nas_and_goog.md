The "User" tab displays information about currently created users, including their usernames, descriptions, roles, statuses, and edit actions. Double-click a username or the operation button to edit user details.

For users with email addresses filled in, their email addresses will be displayed.

Click the "Add" button to "Create/Invite/Import" new users.
