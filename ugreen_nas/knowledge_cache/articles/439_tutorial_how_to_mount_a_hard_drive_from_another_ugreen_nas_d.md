# [Tutorial] How to Mount a Hard Drive from Another UGREEN NAS Device as External Storage in UGOS Pro?

> **Article ID**: `439`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Storage > FAQ > [Tutorial] How to Mount a Hard Drive from Another UGREEN NAS Device as External Storage in UGOS Pro?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/439  

---

In the UGOS Pro operating system of UGREEN NAS, you can use hard drives as external storage for file reading and writing in scenarios such as data migration, data recovery, system maintenance, and backup. The following will provide a detailed introduction to the applicable scenarios and operation steps.

## Main Applicable Scenario

● **Data Migration:** When you need to **migrate data from one UGREEN NAS device to another**, you can mount the hard drive as external storage for easy and quick data transfer.

## Operation Steps to Mount a Hard Drive as External Storage

1. Log in to the UGOS Pro system and go to [**Storage] > [Hard Drive] > [HDD/SSD]"**.

2. Select the hard drive you want to mount as external storage, which is displayed as unused by default. Click on the **"'···'' > Use"** option on the right side.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-09-17/12b9ff19958243f4bfacec197fa6533e.webp)

3. In the pop-up "Use hard drive" window, choose "**External storage**" to use the hard drive as an external storage device. Note: If a message prompts that there is not enough storage, refer to ["Hard Drive Used as External Storage Prompts 'Insufficient Disk Space' – What to Do?"](https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/eyJjb2RlIjoiMiYmNDQwIn0=)for related solutions.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-09-17/c4aefc39db8247b3b22d97617ed73f90.webp)

4. Follow the system prompts and enter your password to verify your identity.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250917/700d8eca-86c9-40fd-9e36-0f5a6fd1ac4a.png)

5. After successful mounting, you can see the mounted external storage device on the "External storage" page.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-09-17/3d07bda51b644df9b6380af95e639a95.webp)

6. You can access the files in the external storage in two ways:

**Method 1:** On the [External storage] page, click the [···] icon next to the external storage device and select "Open".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-09-17/85671e6137904904ab3006f4847c7518.webp)

**Method 2:** You can also go directly to the [Files] > [Peripheral] page to view and manage files stored in the external storage.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-09-17/3757774ac5b44179929ba3a521a09816.webp)

**Notes:**

1. If you no longer need the external storage device, it is recommended to safely eject the hard drive. You can select the corresponding hard drive on the [**External Storage**] page and click [···] > [Remove]" to ensure data safety.

2. When transferring data from the external storage device to the internal storage space of the UGOS Pro system, always **use "Copy"** instead of "Cut" and "Move To". Currently, the UGOS Pro system does not support the resume feature for "Cut" and "Move To" operations.
