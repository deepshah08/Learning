# [FAQ] How to Create a Shared Library on UGREEN NAS to Share Music with Family?

> **Article ID**: `463`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Music > [FAQ] How to Create a Shared Library on UGREEN NAS to Share Music with Family?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/463  

---

If you want to share music files from your UGREEN NAS with family and friends, you can achieve this through the **Shared Library** feature in the UGOS Pro system. Follow these steps to invite others to enjoy your music collection together.

## Steps

1. **Enable Shared Library**: Go to the [Music] > [Settings] page and enable the "Shared library" feature.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-09-17/41e5456930a54b04938808502392ae51.webp)

2. **Select Music Folders**: After enabling the Shared Library, click the **"Add"** button. Select the folders containing music files from your shared folders to be used as resource folders for the Shared Library.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-09-17/f8bedb636ed7412d82091ff99195ffbb.webp)

3. **Set Access Permissions**: You can set different access permissions for each user to control their access level to the Shared Library:

**Access denied**: Users cannot view or play music.

**Play Only**: Users cannot view or play music.

**Editable**: Users can edit music tags or delete music files from the Shared Library.

4. Under View and Play Options, select "Personal library and shared library" to synchronize the data of the Shared Library and display it in your personal Library, allowing easy access and playback of shared music resources with your family.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-09-17/9f34aad4f30d4df6a21717c37a161d4d.webp)

5. Once the setup is complete, you can jointly manage the Library with members who have edit permissions and share songs from the Shared Library.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-09-17/2bdeb98e63054c6791f423b0725ca7b3.webp)
