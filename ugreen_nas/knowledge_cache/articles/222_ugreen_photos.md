# **App Overview**

The "Photos" app helps you easily manage and share all your photos. You can quickly find precious moments, categorize people in your photos, and create personalized albums. Maintain this collection with your family and friends to preserve your memories for the long term.

**You can use "Photos" to:**

**Efficiently Manage Photos and Albums**

* Automatically generate albums by grouping photos based on face and object recognition, location, tags, file type, and recording time/date.
* Provide personalized photo storage management or create shared spaces for collaboration.
* Support multidimensional filtering, such as by time and date, camera model, lens, etc., to quickly find the photos you need.

**Access Your Memories Anytime, Anywhere**

* Share beautiful moments instantly by setting album access permissions to safely share photos with family and friends.
* Use the mobile backup feature to sync all images from your personal phone to UGREEN NAS, freeing up device space while ensuring your content is preserved.

## **Supported Formats in Photos**

**Popular RAW Formats from Major Cameras:** RAW, CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, RW2, PEF, DNG, 3FR, ORF, ICO, PSD, HIF

**Common Image Formats:** JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, WEBP, HEIC, HEIF

**Common Video Formats:** MP4, MOV, WMV, FLV, AVI, AVCHD, WebM, MKV, ASF, MPG, RM, RMVB, Match3gp

## **Feature Support & Device Model Notes**
