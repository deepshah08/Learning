The Theater homepage adopts a high-definition **Poster Wall** layout, featuring **large carousel posters** to showcase curated video content and create an immersive viewing experience. Below is a detailed introduction of its functions.

## Poster Wall and Carousel

Once you **create a library and import video files**, the Theater uses **TMDB metadata scraping** (at least two movies required) to **automatically match** HD posters and complete video information (director, cast, synopsis, etc.), giving your personal media library a professional touch.  
The carousel can be customized to display **Recent Updates**, **My Favorites**, or a specified **library**, creating a personalized, cinema-style portal.

## Categories and Quick Access

In addition to the carousel, the homepage also provides:

● **Recently Played**: Quickly revisit and continue watching your last viewed titles. Playback history can be deleted.

● **Recent Added**: View newly added content in your library.

● **Multi-dimensional Categories**: Video resources are organized by **Folder**, **Library**, **Movies**, **TV Series**, and more, helping you efficiently locate the content you're interested in.

## Navigation Menu

Apart from direct browsing on the homepage, the left-side **navigation menu** provides the following:

● **Recently Played**: Quickly review recently played titles and continue watching.

● **My Favorites**: View your favorited Movies and TV Series.

● **Collection**: Group movie or series collections for easier management.

● **Folder**: Directly access video files from library folders.

● **Library**: Browse content by individual library.

● **Categories**: Filter videos by type.

You can customize the navigation menu in "**Settings" > "Personal"** to display only what you prefer, creating a personalized Theater interface.

## Library

The homepage displays **Library cards**, with covers automatically generated based on recently added content. The cover quality depends on the poster images of the items in the library. Each card supports **editing its cover.** Once uploaded, the new cover will sync across PC and mobile.

### Change Library Cover

1. On the homepage, locate the library you want to modify. **Right-click the cover** and select "**Upload cover"**.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-09-12/024734e557034c9aa6eef0db92d93856.webp)

2. Choose a cover image from your computer, crop it, and click "**OK"** to save.

**Note:** Right-click the library cover and select "**Restore default cover"** to revert to the system-generated version.

### Library Details Page

Click a library cover to enter its details page, where you can:

● **Filter**: Filter content by type, year, status, and other criteria.

● **Sort**: Sort by time, name, and other dimensions.

● **Switch View**: Toggle freely between list view and card view.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-09-12/ff8336f7ae7647389c12f853984dc67c.webp)

## Categories

The Categories module provides refined organization for video content, making it easier to locate specific types of resources. Within this module, all titles under a collection are displayed, but the collection itself is not.

● **All**: Aggregates all visible video resources.

● **Movies**: Displays visible movie titles.

● **TV Series**: Displays visible TV series.

● **Others**: Displays all unrecognized content.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-09-12/1fc60454929145bf9691c1ba1a4f1f07.webp)

## Search

A prominent search button is located in the upper-right corner of the page, allowing you to enter keywords at any time to quickly locate related video resources. The app also remembers previous search terms for faster future queries.

## Batch Operations

The Theater supports **bulk management** of videos, letting you apply actions to multiple files or episodes at once, including:

● **Batch Play**: Select multiple videos and click the "**Play"** button in the upper-right corner for continuous playback.

● **Batch Add to My Favorites**: Add multiple titles to "**My Favorites"**.

● **Batch Add to Collection or Series**: Organize video resources.

● **Batch Delete**: Deleted files can be recovered from the "**Recycle Bin"** in the "**Files"** app.

**Steps:**

1. Hover over a poster card and select multiple videos.

2. Click the "**Play"** button in the upper-right corner to batch play.

3. Click the "**···"** button to perform actions such as add to favorites, add to collection/series, or delete.

**Note:** Deleted files can be recovered from the recycle bin in the "**Files"**.
