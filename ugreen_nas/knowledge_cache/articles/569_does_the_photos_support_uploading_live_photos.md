# Does the Photos Support Uploading Live Photos?

> **Article ID**: `569`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Photos > FAQ > Does the Photos Support Uploading Live Photos?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/569  

---

> The content and screenshots in this article are based on UGOS Pro system firmware version 1.6.0.2861 and Photos app version 1.6.0.1289.Interface and features may vary across firmware versions. Please refer to your actual device display.

The Album in UGOS Pro supports lossless uploading of Live Photos in HEIF format and preserves the original quality when downloading.

## **Supported format and display effect**

**Supported format:** Live Photos, consisting of a still image and a video, can be uploaded in HEIF format.

**After uploading:** Live Photos will show a live icon to help distinguish dynamic images from static ones.

## **Supplementary Notes**

**1.Composition of Live Photos**  
A Live Photo consists of a still image and a short video. To ensure proper playback, both files must be present. If either part is missing, the Live Photo will not display correctly.  
**Note:** The current Windows system does not natively support viewing HEIF format images. You need to use third-party applications that support HEIF or convert the files to other formats to view the live effect.

**2.Opening HEIF Photos from Files**  
When opening HEIF photos directly from the files, you cannot view the Live Photo’s dynamic effect. Currently, the live effect can only be experienced when viewing Live Photos in the album.

**3.Format Conversion During Upload**  
Due to iOS system limitations, when uploading via photo request links, HEIC files (the Live Photo format on iOS devices) are automatically converted to JPEG format. This means files uploaded to the UGREEN NAS Album will be static JPEG images, and the video part will not be uploaded.

**4.No Support for 360-degree Photos and Videos**  
Currently, UGREEN NAS Album does not support uploading or displaying 360-degree photos and videos. If your files contain 360-degree images or video content, they may not upload or display correctly.
