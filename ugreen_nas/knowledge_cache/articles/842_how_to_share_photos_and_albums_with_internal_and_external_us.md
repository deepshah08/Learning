# How to Share Photos and Albums with Internal and External Users

> **Article ID**: `842`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Photos > How to Share Photos and Albums with Internal and External Users`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/842  

---

**Applicable platforms**: UGREEN NAS PC client and web browser.

**Applicable versions**: UGOS Pro firmware 1.15.0.0114 and later.

Actual displays may vary depending on system or application versions. Some features may differ across versions; please refer to your actual interface for accuracy.

In "**Photos**," a family library can be created by linking shared folders. Single photos or entire albums can be shared with friends and family who have NAS accounts, and external links can also be generated to share with non-NAS users.

## Family Internal Sharing (Linked Shared Folder)

1. After entering "**Photos**," click the "Settings" icon at the bottom of the sidebar. Find the "**Folder scope**" option, link the folders you want to share, and then click "**Scan**."

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/a048fa8c4e2c4b8bbbb42df903bdb36c.webp)

2. Set User permission by adding users (multi-select supported) and assign other users permissions (View Only / Download / Upload / Manage).

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/addab7aff878431a89a8ad8cb95ccf09.webp)

3. After setting, users with permissions will be able to view the shared files in the Library.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/987135300d7642fba40e5f60bec10e63.webp)

**Note:** Administrator accounts can view all shared files in the Library by default.

## Share Single or Multiple Photos

1. On the "**Library**" page, select one or multiple photos.

2. Tap the "**Share**" icon in the bottom action bar.

3. Choose a sharing method:

● **Internal Sharing**: Share with users who have registered NAS accounts. In the pop-up window, add users and set permissions (View Only, Download, Upload, Manage), then click "**Confirm**."

● **External Link Sharing**: Generate a link to share with non-NAS users. Access permissions, expiration time, and an access password can be set, after which the link can be copied and sent to the recipient.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/df7193a3bdaa427ca00f4a0df76bd582.webp)

## Share an Entire Album

1. On the "**Personal Albums**" page, right-click the target album or open the album details page.

2. Select "**Share**">"**Edit**" or "**External Link Sharing**."

3. Set the permissions and expiration time as needed, then confirm to complete the sharing.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/09b436e449974181ac1b221b4a73a1b7.webp)

## Manage Shared Albums

● On the "**Shared Albums**">"**Shared by me**" page, All shared albums can be viewed. An album can be right-clicked to modify sharing settings, stop sharing, or copy the link.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/8116fb978b9f4050831f908b48e94773.webp)

● Alternatively, go to "**Settings**">"**Sharing management**" to view all shared albums and collections. From there, manage external links by editing them, stopping sharing, or clearing them.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-07/efc6c00cc70b41e78e6f08752b44542a.webp)
