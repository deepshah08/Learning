# How to Set Videos to Automatically Skip Intro and Outro

> **Article ID**: `821`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Theater > How to Set Videos to Automatically Skip Intro and Outro`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/821

## Automatic Skip of Intro and Outro

The Theater app supports intelligently identifying repeated intro and outro segments in series episodes. During playback, the system will automatically skip these segments to provide a smoother viewing experience.

To use this feature, you need to first enable the identification task in the background, then turn on the skip settings in the player interface.

## Step 1: Enable Background Identification Task

1. Go to the "**Settings**" page in Theater, click "**Background Management**" in the left menu, and turn on "**Identify intros & outros**".

2. Check the media libraries you want the system to analyze. The NAS will automatically process these series in the background.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-01/fc1747f07fd64199bc3fb7487b3847cc.webp)

**Note:**

● The system will prioritize recently played episodes, then process other episodes in the order they were added to the library.

● This feature relies on the NAS's local computing power. When there are many episodes, the full identification process may take some time—please be patient.

● After a video is analyzed, white timestamps will appear on its playback progress bar, indicating the detected intro and outro segments.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-01/27aa06294566429a8c0c6f662a590ca0.webp)

## Step 2: Enable in the Player

Once background identification is complete, you can enable the feature during playback.

1. Open the video playback interface, click "**Player Settings**" button, and turn on "**Skip intros & outros**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-01/be2e040cbef14b20bf6106ed9ae8137a.webp)

2. After enabling, you can view the specific time ranges detected by the system in this settings menu.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-01/d4115cc6d9eb4cd6b02871a16f3367c3.webp)

## Playback Behavior

**Intro Skip**:When playback reaches the identified intro segment, the system automatically skips it.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-01/8168770459664b7e9291b8467e6610ac.webp)

**Outro Skip**: When playback reaches the outro segment, a prompt will appear on screen saying "**The outro will be skipped**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-01/1da33380400c44809db54cb5dbd20311.webp)

## Hidden Files Generated by Identification

After the "**Identify intros & outros**" task completes, the system generates hidden files in the same directory as the original video. These files usually have extensions like `.fg`, `.fg.ed`, or `.fg.op`.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-01/70a94d25080e48f3be2e7cd973804a74.webp)

### How to View Hidden Files

These files are hidden by default. In the "**Files**" app settings, uncheck "**Start with (.)**" to display and access them in the directory.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-01/50daf7d6fab243de9ad7b165741add50.webp)

### Effect of Disabling the Feature

If you turn off "**Identify intros & outros**" in the background, any already generated hidden files remain valid and do not affect the skip functionality for existing videos.

### Deleting Files

You can manually delete these hidden files. However, doing so will permanently remove the intro/outro data for the video. To restore it, you must re-enable the identification feature and let the system re-scan the episode.
