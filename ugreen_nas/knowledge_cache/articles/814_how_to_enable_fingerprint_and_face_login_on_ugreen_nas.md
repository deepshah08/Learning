# How to Enable Fingerprint and Face Login on UGREEN NAS?

> **Article ID**: `814`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > UGOS Pro > How to Enable Fingerprint and Face Login on UGREEN NAS?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `MOBILE`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/814  

---

UGREEN NAS supports biometric authentication, allowing you to sign in quickly and securely using facial recognition or fingerprint.

## Device and Version Requirements

To ensure the biometric feature works properly, please make sure your mobile device and NAS meet the following requirements:

**UGREEN NAS firmware:** System version must be updated to 1.14.10 or later.

**iOS devices:**

● Your device must support Face ID, and "**Face ID & Passcode**" must be enabled in system settings.

● The UGREEN NAS app must be updated to V1.13.0 or later.

**Android devices:**

● Your device must support biometric authentication, and fingerprint must be enrolled in system settings (some Samsung devices also support face unlock).

● The UGREEN NAS app must be updated to V1.14.0 or later.

## iOS Device Instructions

**Enable Face ID Login:**

1. After confirming the requirements above, open the UGREEN NAS app. A prompt to enable Face ID login will appear automatically.

2. Tap "**Enable**" and grant permission for the app to use Face ID.

3. Once enabled, future logins will use Face ID verification automatically without requiring a password.

**Disable Face ID Login:**

1. Log in to the UGREEN Nas app and tap your profile avatar in the top-left corner.

2. Tap your "**username**" at the top to enter the local account details page.

3. Find and tap "**Sign in with Face ID**", then select "**Disable ‘Sign in with Face ID’**".

To re-enable it, simply return to this page and turn it on again.

## Android Device Instructions

**Enable Sign in with biometrics:**

1. Log in to the UGREEN NAS app and tap your profile avatar in the top-left corner.

2. Tap your "**username**" at the top to enter the local account details page.

3. Find and tap "**Sign in with biometrics**", select "**Enable**", and follow the on-screen instructions to complete fingerprint or face authentication authorization.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-06-18/28170fa1cc814e949942209635569250.webp)

**Disable Sign in with biometrics:**  
The steps are the same as enabling it—go to the "**Sign in with biometrics**" page and tap the disable option.
