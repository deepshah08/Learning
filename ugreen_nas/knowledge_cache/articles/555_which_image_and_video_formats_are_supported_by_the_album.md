# Which image and video formats are supported by the album?

> **Article ID**: `555`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Photos > FAQ > Which image and video formats are supported by the album?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/555  

---

The album supports uploading the following formats:

**RAW formats from mainstream cameras:** RAW, CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, RW2, PEF, DNG, 3FR, ORF, ICO, PSD

**Common image formats:** JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, WEBP, HEIC, HEIF

**Common video formats:** MP4, MOV, WMV, FLV, AVI, AVCHD, WebM, MKV, ASF, MPG, RM, RMVB

Notes:

1. A Live Photo consists of both a still image and a video, and both must be present for it to play properly.
2. Due to iOS restrictions, HEIC files uploaded via a Photos request link will be automatically converted to JPEG.
3. 360° photos and videos are not currently supported.
