# How to Upload and Download Files Between UGREEN NAS and Quark Drive?

> **Article ID**: `705`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Cloud Drives > How to Upload and Download Files Between UGREEN NAS and Quark Drive?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/705  

---

With the "Cloud Drives", you can easily connect to Quark Drive for fast file uploads and downloads. This article explains how to connect Quark Drive and transfer data between the UGOS Pro system and QuarkDrive.

## How to Connect Quark Cloud Drive to UGREEN NAS?

1. Open the "Cloud Drives"app and click "New Connection".

2. From the list of supported cloud drives, select "Quark Drive", then click "Next".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/7b737d531fb743e49ee87ba4c7df17ef.webp)

3. You will be redirected to the Quark Drive login page. Log in and authorize access using the Quark App QR code or your mobile phone number.

**Note**: If you don't have a Quark Drive account, please download the Quark app on your phone and complete the registration first.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/b238af6f0e9841a1929103dbe6373124.webp)

4. After a successful login, return to the "Overview" page to view the connection status of Quark Drive.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/1ff6b17c2d0b46258b11bdcaedbe8897.webp)

## How to Upload Files from UGREEN NAS to Quark Drive?

1. Click on the connected Quark Drive to view its current storage capacity.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/7f7f765b972647c7bebc786b25dec3b3.webp)

2. On the [Connection] page, under [Upload to Cloud Drive], click "Select file".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/e7d8338b40a9455e9e78056fc560a52c.webp)

3. Choose the save path for the files to be uploaded, then click "Select File "again.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/56186b9c52d74989a9177a41cf1465bb.webp)

4. In the [Select file] window, select the files or folders from "Files" on your UGREEN NAS and click "Comfirm".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/45c4aa411ac5411ca976ca5f1a04e987.webp)

5. During the upload process, you can check progress in the [Upload].

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/25b52f6b9c134a359bd263beba23ad3f.webp)

6. Once the upload is complete, log in to the Quark Cloud Drive website to view the files in the designated folder.

## How to Download Files from Quark Cloud Drive to UGREEN NAS?

1. On the [Connection] page, under [Download toNAS], click "Select file".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/f6638310e1844f99bfe21e0c803e75b5.webp)

2. Choose the files or folders you want to download from Quark Drive, then click "Next".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/3647829697204d47a00b462fb6c3e705.webp)

3. Set the destination path on UGREEN NAS where the files will be saved, then click "Confirm" to start downloading.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/89e07370242e4010836e1d838f916064.webp)

4. During the download process, you can monitor progress in the [Download].

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-22/b00654d67eee4c5fb28e922c75d40cff.webp)

5. After the download is complete, go to "Files" on your UGREEN NAS to find the downloaded files in the selected folder.

## Notes

1. **Network Connection**: Ensure a stable network connection during file transfers to prevent interruptions or data loss.

2. **Storage Space**: Make sure there is sufficient storage space on both the UGREEN NAS and Quark Drive to avoid transfer failures.

3. **Sync Functionality**: Quark Drive currently supports upload and download only; sync functionality is not available.

4. **Concurrent Task Limits**: Quark Drive members can run up to 2 concurrent tasks; non-members are limited to 1.

5. **Transfer Speeds** (actual speed may vary depending on network conditions):

● **Upload**: No significant difference between members and non-members; average speed is around 8MB/s.

● **Download**: Significant difference between members and non-members; members average around 10MB/s, non-members around 100KB/s.

6. **File Size Limits**: Non-members can upload files up to 40GB each; members up to 200GB. Non-members can upload video files (with some restrictions compared to member rules).

The above information is for reference only. Please refer to the official Quark Drive documentation for the most accurate details.
