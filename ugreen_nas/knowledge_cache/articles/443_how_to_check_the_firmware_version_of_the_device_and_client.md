# How to check the firmware version of the device and client?

> **Article ID**: `443`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Control Panel > FAQ > How to check the firmware version of the device and client?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/443  

---

To ensure your UGREEN NAS device and client software are up to date, you can check the system and client version information using the following methods:

## Check the UGOS Pro system version:

**Method 1: Via UGREEN NAS PC client/web interface**

1. Open the UGREEN NAS PC client or log in to the web interface.

2. Go to [Control Panel] > [About].

3. On this page, you can view the current UGOS Pro system version running on your device.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-08/09db69e6b51344c0870c2d3119e75e6b.webp)

**Method 2: Via UGREEN NAS mobile client**

1. Open the UGREEN NAS mobile client.

2. Go to [Control Panel] > [About].

3. You can view the system version information on the displayed page.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-08/3518011b5d5f45b1ad2568db9cbe4255.webp)

## Check the UGREEN NAS client version:

**Method 1: On the PC client**

1. Open the UGREEN NAS PC client.

2. Click on the user avatar in the top right corner.

3. In the dropdown menu, you can view the current client version information.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-08/b88a244cb3444b6a9b7cafd005514f77.webp)

**Method 2: On the mobile client**

1. Open the UGREEN NAS mobile client.

2. Click on the user avatar in the top left corner.

3. Go to the [About] page, where you can view the current mobile client version information.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-08/aebddc78e95345cf8df3445726af81f9.webp)
