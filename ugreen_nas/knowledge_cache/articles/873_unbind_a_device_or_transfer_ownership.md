# Unbind a Device or Transfer Ownership

> **Category**: `Application Guide > UGOS Pro > Unbind a Device or Transfer Ownership`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `COMMON`

---

## Feature Overview

When the UGREEN NAS needs to be sold, given to someone else, or handed over to another administrator, device "**Unbind**" or "**Ownership Transfer**" can be performed through the UGREEN Account portal.

This article is for reference only. The actual interface and operation path may vary slightly due to system or app version updates. Please refer to the actual interface.

## Enter the Device Unbinding Wizard

1. Visit and sign in to [UGREEN Account](https://web.ugnas.com/account/login/#/login) manage center.

2. Go to the "**My devices**" page and find the UGREEN NAS device to unbind in the device list.

3. Click "**…**" on the right side of the device>"**Unbind device**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-06-09/29fe41e43501445f9a3562b35f137937.webp)

4. Identity verification is required for this operation. Follow the prompts to enter **the current account password**, or switch to **mobile verification code** to complete security verification.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-06-09/3f803198601c40939345af7ec836b3b4.webp)

## Select an Action

After identity verification is completed, the system will provide the corresponding action options based on the account binding status of the current device.

**Note:** "**Transfer ownership**" is a condition-triggered feature. This option is displayed only when there is another user with local administrator permissions on the NAS device and that user **has linked a UGREEN Account**. If these conditions are not met, such as when the device has only one administrator or only the current user has linked a UGREEN Account, only device unbinding can be performed.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-06-09/2ea6d4c743b544ea82cbbb59908edcb0.webp)

### Unbind Device

If the device no longer needs to be managed, click "**Unbind**" and complete the secondary unbinding confirmation.

### Transfer Device Ownership

1. Click "**Transfer ownership**" and select a recipient.

2. The system will list users who currently meet the transfer requirements, meaning local administrators who have linked a UGREEN Account. Select the target recipient and click "**Next**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-06-09/4d5e8126b6dd45459c1ae68285a43789.webp)

3. In the confirmation window, click "**Unbind**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-06-09/ace06538979f4662a26deedf7c51f1e4.webp)

Once the operation is complete, your current UGREEN Account will be unbound from the device, and the device will be bound to the selected recipient instead.
