# Does the UGREEN NAS 10-Gigabit network card support auto-negotiation?

> **Article ID**: `656`  
> **Category**: `Troubleshooting > Hardware Failure > Does the UGREEN NAS 10-Gigabit network card support auto-negotiation?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/656  

---

The 10-Gigabit network cards of UGREEN NAS are compatible with various network environments. Regardless of the current network speed, they operate stably and deliver optimal performance. This ensures that users won't need to worry about compatibility issues when upgrading their network equipment in the future.

### **Supported Speeds and Auto-Negotiation**

The following models of UGREEN 10-Gigabit network cards support a range of network speeds. The card will automatically adjust to the optimal speed based on the current network environment — no manual configuration is required.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supported 10GbE Network Card Models | Supported Speeds | Auto-Negotiation |
| DXP480T Plus | 10Gbps, 5Gbps, 2.5Gbps, 1Gbps, 100Mbps | Yes |
| DXP4800 Plus | 10Gbps, 5Gbps, 2.5Gbps, 1Gbps, 100Mbps | Yes |
| DXP6800 Series | 10Gbps, 5Gbps, 2.5Gbps, 1Gbps, 100Mbps | Yes |
| DXP8800 Series | 10Gbps, 5Gbps, 2.5Gbps, 1Gbps, 100Mbps | Yes |

### **Usage Tips**

1. If you plan to upgrade your network in the future (e.g., switching to a high-speed router or 10GbE switch), it’s recommended to prepare devices that support gigabit or 10-gigabit speeds in advance. This ensures your 10GbE network card can perform at its full potential.
2. Make sure that all intermediate devices between your NAS and computer/other devices (such as routers and switches) support at least gigabit speeds. If any one of these devices has a lower speed, it may bottleneck and slow down the entire network.
