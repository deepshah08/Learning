# How to Share Libraries and Albums with Family Members?

> **Article ID**: `904`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Photos > How to Share Libraries and Albums with Family Members?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`

## Applicability

**Applicable Client**: UGREEN NAS PC Client (Windows/macOS), Web Browser

**Applicable Version**: UGOS Pro Firmware 1.17.0.0095 or later

The descriptions in this document are for reference only. The actual interface may vary depending on system or app updates, please refer to the actual interface.

## Introduction

In a family, each member may have a large collection of photos and videos stored separately on their phones, making it difficult to view and revisit them together. With the "**Photos**" app, you can create a shared library or shared album to bring your family's photos and videos together for easy viewing and management.

This article introduces two sharing methods. You can choose the one that best suits your needs:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Method** | **Use Case** | **Key feature** |
| **Shared Library** | Backing up the whole family's photos to the same folder | Brings all photos together in one library, where members can view all content |
| **Shared Album** | Sharing an existing themed album with family members, such as "Travel Memories" or "Family Gatherings" | Shares only the selected album without affecting other content in the library |

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure the following requirements are met:

● A separate UGOS Pro system account has been created for each family member.

● Family members have installed the UGREEN NAS PC client or can access UGREEN NAS through a web browser.

## Create a Family Library by Linking a Shared Folder

This method is ideal for families who want to store their photos in the same shared folder. All family members can view all photos and videos in this folder from the library.

### Create a Shared Folder and Set Permissions

1. Open "**Files**", click the "**+**" icon on the toolbar, and select "**Create shared folder**". In the pop-up window, enter the folder name and configure the relevant settings.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/e3f73669871b4368adc80d86c6f55d4e.webp)

2. Click "**Create**". The "**Shared folder permissions**" window will appear automatically. Set "**Read/Write**" permission for all family members so they can upload photos and videos from their phones to this folder.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/b2c3ec1658ec4feea50dfb9369b1f40b.webp)

### Add the Folder to Photos

1. Open the "**Photos**" app and go to "**Settings**" > "**Folder scope**" > "**Shared Folder**". Click the "**+**" icon to add the shared folder you created.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/4f463963428048c7be36a876294d2a2b.webp)

2. After the folder is added, click the "**Add user**" button (the "**+**" icon), select all family members, and grant them "**Manage**" permission. This allows each member to view and manage all photos and videos in the shared folder from the library.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/3ac764b16534485dae30fd236739ffd8.webp)

After completing the steps above, all family members can view and manage family photos and videos together in the library of the "**Photos**" app.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/6174be2fd69643e3b6cb4b2ab18178e3.webp)

## Share an Existing Album Using Internal Sharing

If you have already created a personal album, such as "Family Gatherings" or "Travel Memories", and want to share it with family members who have UGOS Pro system accounts for viewing or collaborative management, you can use the internal sharing feature.

1. Open the "**Photos**" app and click "**Personal Albums**" in the left sidebar.

2. Hover over the album you want to share, right-click it, and select "**Share**" > "**Edit internal sharing**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/f4b06387d2b14e36b6f9843c64e514e6.webp)

3. In the pop-up window, click the "**+**" button to add users, then select the family members you want to share the album with.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/3bee0d6289994e909f61098d2491a027.webp)

4. Set the appropriate permissions for each family member based on your needs (You can also set permissions in batches):

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/e6d76e8152b04bcba3cf7c8ced99277b.webp)

● **View only**: Can only view photos in the album.

● **Download**: Can view and download photos.

● **Upload**: Can view, download, and upload photos.

● **Manage**: Has full permissions, including adding and deleting photos.

5. After confirming the settings, simply exit the window for the changes to take effect.

After family members sign in to their own UGOS Pro accounts, they can view or manage the album under "**Shared Albums**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-25/7508d64caac04a97a2d6fd454fdbeec0.webp)
