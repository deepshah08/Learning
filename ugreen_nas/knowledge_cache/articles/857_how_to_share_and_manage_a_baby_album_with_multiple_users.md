# How to Share and Manage a Baby Album with Multiple Users?

> **Article ID**: `857`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Photos > How to Share and Manage a Baby Album with Multiple Users?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/857  

---

**Applicable models**: DH series, DX series, DXP series, iDX series

**Applicable clients**: UGREEN Cloud PC client, Web client

**Applicable version**: UGOS Pro firmware 1.17.0.0095 or later

The screenshots in this article are for reference only. The actual interface may vary slightly depending on the system or app version, please refer to the actual interface.

## Scenario Description

In a family scenario, to enable multiple users to share and collaboratively manage a Baby Album, you can use features such as **People recognition**, **Sharing permissions**, and **Folder linking** to bring together photos taken by different family Member (e.g., parents, grandparents) into a single, unified Baby Album.

It can be implemented in two ways, depending on your needs:

## Prerequisites

● **The administrator has created a Baby album**: You can refer to "[How to Create and Manage Baby Albums](https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/eyJjb2RlIjoiMiYmODQwIiwiY2xpZW50VHlwZSI6IiJ9) ", and make sure the baby's profile photo has been linked.

● **Enable People recognition**: Each family member should go to "**Settings**" **>** "**AI settings**" in their own account, enable the "**People recognition**" model, and wait for the system to identify and group the baby’s photos.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/18d71e8fe5424cbcbc8b4c79c4814f0d.webp)

## Method 1: Shared management via adding baby’s profile or personal album folders

This method is suitable when each family member has their own personal photo library (with photos stored in individual personal folders). By sharing the Baby Album and adding the baby’s profile, photos can be automatically aggregated.

1. **The administrator creates a Baby Album and adds family Member as managers**   
In "**Personal Albums**", open the created Baby Album (e.g., "Baby’s Growth Diary"), click the "**Add user**" ("**+**") button, add family member as shared Member of the Baby Album, and set their permission to "**Manage**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/2ba331787d5c4e58928cda8c2e13db2e.webp)

2. **Family Member add the baby’s profile or folders to the shared Baby Album**

● Family Member log in to their own accounts, go to "**Shared Albums**" **>** "**Shared by others**", and find the Baby Album shared by the administrator.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/791bdb398a5b401eb4dbc23279d0af20.webp)

● Go to the album details page, tap the "**…**" icon, and select "**Edit baby album**" > "**Add baby**". Then select the corresponding baby profile from your People Recognition list.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/ebc99a1724d347bb873a02ada50e0ad0.webp)

● (Optional) You can also click "**Add folder**", then select a personal or shared folder on the NAS that contains baby photos and has been linked to the album.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/74262b3a6c594feca6eaf98d8efa432b.webp)

3. Click "**Confirm**". The system will automatically sync all photos associated with the linked baby profile and folder to the Baby Album.

**Note**: Family members can also tap "**Upload**" on the Baby Album details page to upload additional photos from their phone or personal library. These photos are saved directly to the folder linked to the Baby Album, but they are not added to the uploader's People Categorization.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/62d973a6d97c4ad382561a718538b2dc.webp)

## Method 2: Centralized album management via shared folders

This method is ideal when all family members upload their photos to a shared folder. The administrator sets this folder as the default storage location for the album, allowing everyone to view all photos and videos in the folder under Library, while the administrator centrally creates and manages the Baby Album.

1. **The administrator creates a shared folder and configures user permissions**

In "**Files**", create a shared folder (e.g., Family Baby Album) and set up shared folder permissions for all family Member, enabling "**Read/Write**" access (making it easier to upload photos and videos from their phone.).

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/0cfb8d3590eb4bbfa4279795a1ec1df1.webp)

2. **The administrator links the shared folder to the album and sets it as default**

● Open the Photos app, go to "**Settings**" **>** "**Folder scope**" **>** "**Shared Folder**", and tap "**+**" to add the shared folder.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/0f725150d45d4fe69c8bc460fb0d4e56.webp)

After linking the shared family album folder, continue by tapping the "**Add user**" icon, selecting all family Member, and granting "**Manage**"permissions (to allow viewing and managing shared photos and videos in the media library), or set different permissions based on actual family needs.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/9b78b71cc496465cacd95e320cc5cf32.webp)

● After setup is complete, tap the "**…**" icon on the right side of the folder and select "**Set as fefault folder**". All photos uploaded by any member through the library will be saved to this shared folder by default.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/b96041380dfc4b299d80809fd7f9c370.webp)

3. **Family members upload photos from their phone albums to a shared folder**

Family members open the UGREEN NAS App on their phones, go to the Library page in the "**Photos**" app, tap the "**+**" icon, and select "**Upload**". Then, they can upload baby photos stored on their phones to the default shared folder created by the administrator.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/feace3c8bb274601ae3f60779567bfc7.webp)

4. **The administrator creates a Baby Album and shares it with family Member**

● The administrator creates a Baby Album in "**Personal Albums**", and links the baby’s profile or adds folders.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/6102cb8062d04dea8a92b7867545fc24.webp)

● Share the album with all family Member by using "**Add family member**", and set their permissions to "**Manage**"**.**

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/3275abbffcba443991c6bd4995556579.webp)

● All family member can then view and manage all baby photos in a unified way via the library or Shared Albums.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-24/5f037428aef446068552c04b96f8ea56.webp)

## Additional Notes

● If multiple family member add the same baby profile (e.g., all recognize "Q Baby"), the system will automatically deduplicate entries to prevent the same photo from appearing multiple times in the album.

● If the system fails to correctly recognize the baby’s profile, refer to"[How to Intelligently Categorize People in Photos?](https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/eyJjb2RlIjoiMiYmNTY3IiwiY2xpZW50VHlwZSI6IiJ9) "to manually merge, rename, or lock profiles.

● Standard users cannot modify shared folder scope settings. Please contact the administrator for any adjustments.
