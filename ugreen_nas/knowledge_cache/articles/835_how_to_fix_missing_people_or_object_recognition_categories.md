# How to Fix Missing People or Object Recognition Categories

> **Article ID**: `835`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Photos > FAQ > How to Fix Missing People or Object Recognition Categories`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `MOBILE`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/835  

---

### Issue Description

When you open the Photos app in the UGREEN NAS and switch to the "**Categorization**" page, the "**People**" or "**Object Recognition**" sections may not appear. This is usually because the underlying AI recognition models have not been enabled.

### Solution

Before using any classification features, make sure the AI recognition models are enabled:

1. Open the Photos app and tap "**Settings**" in the bottom navigation bar to enter the global settings page.

2. In the settings list, find and tap "**Intelligent**" to access the configuration panel for AI recognition features.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-29/9c1e9eef66e94c3895de459eec694ead.webp)

3. Turn on the switches on the right for the following features (blue indicates enabled):

○ **People recognition**: Automatically detects facial features in photos and videos and groups them accordingly. The page will also display the number of photos pending recognition in real time. Tap "**Advanced settings**" below for more detailed control over the recognition process.

○ **Text recognition**: Automatically extracts text from photos (OCR). Once enabled, you can quickly find images by searching for keywords in the text (such as IDs or invoice details).

○ **Similar/Duplicate photo recognition**: Automatically scans in the background and tags visually similar or identical photos, providing data for the "**Similar & Duplicate**" cleanup feature.

○ **Pet recognition**: Identifies 39 common cat and dog breeds (e.g., Bulldog, Shiba Inu, British Shorthair) and groups them automatically.

○ **Sensitive content recognition**: Automatically identifies sensitive images (such as explicit content) and applies blur by default when browsing the album list to protect your privacy and viewing experience.

○ **Image recognition**: Deeply understands image content and scene elements, enabling "**text-based image search**" for the album and providing underlying technical support for automatic scene and object classification.

**Notes**:

● After enabling these models, the system will scan your "**Library**" using local computing power. If you have a large number of photos, the initial analysis may take some time.

● If you notice missed or incorrect recognition, or after bulk photo imports, you can tap "**Recognize again**" below the feature to rescan the entire Photos.
