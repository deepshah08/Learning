# How to Check if Your Computer and UGREEN NAS are on the Same LAN

> **Article ID**: `388`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Control Panel > FAQ > How to Check if Your Computer and UGREEN NAS are on the Same LAN`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/388  

---

When the client connection is abnormal or the device cannot be found, you can use this method to check the network status. If they are not on the same LAN, connect both the UGREEN NAS device and the computer to the same router via Ethernet cable, then try again.

## Method 1: Check Via the Client

● **Logged-in client:** You can view the device connection status in the top menu bar of the system desktop.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-27/3c8d7f5df3044d8d85d6250a812fa6bb.webp)

● **Not logged in:** On the client login page, click "More Connections" to open the connection window. In the "LAN devices" list, you can see the detected UGREEN NAS devices. If the devices are found, it indicates that the computer and the NAS are currently on the same LAN.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-27/e50242b029fe49d2a8e6651ae8e0a897.webp)

## Method 2: Access Via Browser

1. Open a browser and enter **find.ugnas.com** in the address bar.

2. The system will automatically search for UGREEN NAS devices on the LAN.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-08-27/5b993746354448758e18092bde7cf09a.webp)
