## Introduction

**Photos** helps you easily organize photos and videos, share your moments with family and friends anytime. The app provides a variety of practical features, including quick photo search, automatic organization, custom album creation, and convenient sharing and collaboration with others.

**Main features include**:

● **Quick Filtering**: Supports smart search and filtering by multiple criteria, such as file type, shooting date, and device, helping you quickly find the content you need.

● **Intelligent organization**: Automatically groups photos into smart albums based on features such as people, locations, pets, and objects. You can also create personalized albums with specific themes to meet different organization needs.

● **Custom Categories**: Supports creating personalized AI categories. The system can identify and categorize related images in your photo library based on category names or custom-trained models, enabling more accurate classification.

● **Flexible Sharing and Collaboration**: Allows you to set sharing permissions for photos or albums and invite family members, friends, or team members to manage the photo library together, making it easy to share precious memories anytime, anywhere.

● **Granular Permission Management**: Supports setting different permissions for members, including viewing, downloading, uploading, and management permissions. This helps protect data security while improving collaboration efficiency for families or teams.

● **Automatic Backup**: Supports one-click backup of photos and videos from mobile devices to the NAS Photos library. It helps free up device storage space, securely preserve important memories, and provides centralized management of your photo collection.

## Supported File Formats

● RAW formats from mainstream cameras: RAW, CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, RW2, PEF, DNG, 3FR, ORF, ICO, PSD, HIF.

● Common image formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, WEBP, HEIC, HEIF.

● Common video formats: MP4, MOV, WMV, FLV, AVI, AVCHD, WebM, MKV, ASF, MPG, RM, RMVB, 3GP.

## Installation and Access

1. Open "**App Center**" and find the "**Photos**" app.

2. Click "**Install**" and follow the system wizard to complete the installation settings.

3. After installation, click the Photos icon on the desktop or in the App Library to start using the app.
