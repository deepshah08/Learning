### **Q1: Where can I open the toolbar?**

**A1:** You can open the toolbar by long-pressing a movie card in the [Recently Updated & My Favorites] section on the homepage, or by tapping the [···] button on the movie details page.

### **Q2: What are the differences between the scanning methods?**

**A2:** In the Theater, there are three different scanning methods available to help ensure that all your media files are correctly identified and managed. These methods are [Scan Newly Added and Modified], [Scan All and Fill in Missing], and [Scan All and Replace]. Here is a detailed explanation of each:

1. **[Scan Newly Added and Modified]**

This method focuses on quickly updating the library by identifying newly added or recently modified files.

* **Use case:** Ideal when you only want to include newly added or updated media files. It skips previously scanned and unmodified files, making the process faster.
* **Pros:** Saves time and resources by scanning only new or changed files.
* **Cons:** Files that haven't been changed will not be rescanned or updated.

2. **[Scan All and Fill in Missing]**

This method scans the entire library and fills in any missing file information.

* **Use case:** Best for situations where you want to ensure all files are fully scanned and up to date, especially when some files were previously missed or not properly recognized.
* **Pros:** Ensures library completeness by supplementing any missing data.
* **Cons:** More time-consuming than the first method but less intensive than a full replace.

3. **[Scan All and Replace]**

This method performs a full rescan of the entire library, replacing all existing file information based on your metadata retrieval settings.

* **Use case:** Recommended when the library has major errors, inconsistencies, or requires a complete refresh.
* **Pros:** Fully updates the library by rescanning and replacing all existing data, ensuring consistency.
* **Cons:** The most time- and resource-intensive method due to the full rescan and replacement process.

4. **Summary**

Each scanning method serves a specific purpose and comes with its own advantages and drawbacks. Choosing the appropriate method based on your needs can help you efficiently manage and maintain your library:

* **For quick updates:** Choose [Scan Newly Added and Modified].
* **To ensure completeness:** Choose [Scan All and Fill in Missing].
* **For a full update:** Choose [Scan All and Replace].
* **To apply new metadata source settings:** Choose [Scan All and Replace].

Select the method that best matches your current needs to keep your library accurate and complete.

### **Q3: Why does manually edited video information/metadata disappear?**

A3: When you choose [Scan All and Replace], the system refreshes all metadata based on the metadata retrieval settings configured in the library. As a result, any manually edited video information or metadata will be overwritten and replaced by the system-generated metadata.
