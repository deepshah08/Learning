# Guide to Switching Accounts on PC/Mobile Client

> **Article ID**: `695`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > UGOS Pro > Guide to Switching Accounts on PC/Mobile Client`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `COMMON`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/695  

---

The UGOS Pro system supports switching between multiple device accounts on both PC and mobile clients, making it convenient for users who own multiple UGREEN NAS devices or have multiple accounts. You can quickly switch to another device or account without logging out of the current one. The steps are as follows:

## **Switch Account on the PC Client**

1. Open the UGREEN NAS PC client, click the "user avatar" icon in the upper right corner, and select [Switch Account].

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250604/556fdcd2-c79c-41bc-af40-d53d7168d05a.png)

2. In the [Switch Account] window:

* To switch to an existing device account, select it and click "Switch to this account";
* To add a new account, click "Log in to another account", then enter the new device account credentials to complete the login.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250604/a5807950-9fba-4b99-b4ac-81b4d549c36d.png)

## **Switch Account on Mobile App**

1. Open the UGREEN NAS mobile app, tap the "User Avatar" icon in the top-left corner, and select [Switch Account].

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250604/121ef5fd-8f07-49ae-889a-09aa1e8c371d.png)

2. On the [Switch Account] page, tap the avatar of an already logged-in account to quickly switch.
3. To add a new account, tap the "`+`" button on the page to add a new account.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250604/59bff8da-8f95-45d4-8696-552bdf68f111.png)
