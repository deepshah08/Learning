## Applicability

**Applicable Client**: UGREEN NAS PC Client, Web Browser

**Applicable Version**: UGOS Pro Firmware 1.17.0.0095 or later

The descriptions in this document are for reference only. The interface may vary depending on system or app updates, please refer to the actual interface.

## Introduction

In the "**Photos**" app, you can create a photo upload invitation link and share it with family members or friends. Even without a NAS account, they can directly upload photos to the specified album or folder through the link. You can also set the link expiration date, storage location, access password, and other settings to easily collect photos while effectively protecting your privacy.

## Invite Friends to Upload Photos

1. Open the "**Photos**" app. On the top toolbar, click the "**+**" button and select "**Invite friends to upload**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-27/795eac86cf1c430fb690627b6edf525b.webp)

2. In the pop-up window, configure the following options as needed:

● **Edit link name**: Modify the invitation link name and add a note.

● **Validity**: Set the validity period of the invitation link (for example, 7 days).

● **Add to album (Optional)**: Select a target album. Uploaded photos will be automatically added to the specified album for easier browsing.

● **Storage location**: Select the storage path for photos uploaded by friends.

● **Password**: It is recommended to enable and set a password to protect your privacy and data security.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-27/7553094647d94f1aad665a49ebdd8cd7.webp)

3. After completing the configuration, click "**Create and copy link**" and send the generated link and password to your family members or friends. They can open the link in a browser and upload photos without logging in or installing the client.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-27/3fb39119a6f84ce0b67c3eeb3e741cda.webp)

## Share an Existing Album Externally

1. On the "**Personal Albums**" page, right-click the album you want to share, or open the album details page, then select "**Share**" > "**Edit external sharing**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-27/d4739a49fcf444f3aee8b01a1ba5247e.webp)

2. Set the permissions, validity period, password, and other options as needed. Click "**Create share**", then copy the link and send it to your friends.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-27/df9d413d1b914df389857fad15fe49ee.webp)

Recipients do not need to sign in or install the client. They can open the link directly to view or download photos in the album.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-27/964ba4ff6e8744cb87e5389a7fb6febb.webp)

## Manage Created Invitation Links

You can view and manage created invitation links at any time, and edit or delete them as needed.

1. Click the "**Settings**" icon and select "**Friend upload management**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-27/7da7ab2208b2462b968ca86d3add4399.webp)

2. On the Friend upload management page, you can perform the following operations:

● **Clear Invalid links**: Click to delete all expired invitation links.

● **Sort and view**: Sort the link list by "**Sharing time**" or "**Validity**".

● **Edit links**: Copy the link address. Click the "**...**" icon on the right side of a link to modify the link name, note, validity period, photo storage location, and access password, or delete the invitation link.

## Manage Shared Albums

Go to "**Settings**" > "**Sharing management**" to view all shared albums and collections. From there, you can edit shared external links, stop sharing, or clear sharing records.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-27/070b962fb2b64b3ba37749ad7ee134e5.webp)

## Notes

● After a link expires, the system will automatically mark it as “Expired” but will not delete it immediately, allowing you to view the history. You can manually clear expired links.

● When creating a friend upload invitation link, you can specify the folder where uploaded photos will be saved (the default is the folder associated with the album). You can also change the save location when editing the link on the management page.
