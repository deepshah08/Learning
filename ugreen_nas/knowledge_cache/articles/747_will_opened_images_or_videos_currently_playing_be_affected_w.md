# Will opened images or videos currently playing be affected when the Vault is locked?

> **Article ID**: `747`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Vault > Will opened images or videos currently playing be affected when the Vault is locked?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/747  

---

## Issue Description

Will locking the Vault interrupt images that are currently open or videos that are playing?

## Cause Analysis

The Vault locking mechanism mainly restricts file access and operation permissions and does not forcibly interrupt content already loaded by the system.

When an image viewer or video player opens a file, the data is loaded into memory. If the Vault is locked afterward, the already loaded content can still be used normally.

## Solution

Opened images and videos currently playing will not be affected and can continue to be viewed or played.

However, after the Vault is locked, you will no longer be able to create, copy, move, or reopen files inside the Vault until it is unlocked again.
