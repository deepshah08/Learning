# How to Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) to Enhance System Security?

## Applicability

**Applicable Client**: UGREEN NAS PC Client (Windows/macOS), Web Browser

**Applicable Version**: UGOS Pro Firmware 1.16.0.0042 or later

The descriptions in this document are for reference only. The interface may vary depending on system or app updates, please refer to the actual interface.

## Introduction

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is a feature that enhances account security. Once enabled, you need to enter both your password and a one-time password (OTP) to sign in. This helps prevent unauthorized access to your account. Even if someone obtains your username and password, they still need the one-time password generated by the OTP app on your phone to access your account.

**Note**:

● Enabling 2FA enhances account and system security and helps prevent unauthorized access.

● OTP codes are generated by an authenticator app on your phone. You can use any app that supports the OTP protocol, such as Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator.

## Prerequisites

Before enabling two-factor authentication (2FA), complete the following preparations.

### Download and Install an OTP App

1. Search for and install **Microsoft Authenticator** from your phone's app store, or install another app that supports the OTP protocol (such as **Google Authenticator**).

2. Microsoft official download page: [Download Microsoft Authenticator](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/mobile-authenticator-app)

### Ensure Time Synchronization

OTP codes are time-sensitive, so the time on your phone and NAS must be synchronized.

● **Device**: Open "**Control Panel**", click "**Time & Language**" > "**Time**", and select "**Auto sync with time server**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-28/f8b9dc97632e4a1b9b6f6ef299dd81ae.webp)

● **Phone**: Make sure your phone’s system time is set to sync automatically.

**Note**: If the time is not synchronized, OTP codes may be invalid. Make sure the time on both devices is synchronized.

## Enable Two-Factor Authentication

1. Click the user icon in the upper-right corner of the UGOS Pro desktop, select your username, and go to the "**Local account settings**" page.

2. Select "**Account security**", find "**Two-factor authentication (2FA)**", and click the "**Enable**" button.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-28/5cb91ad05ae343e9b0b8ea52bad16b6b.webp)

3. In the verification pop-up, enter the password for the currently signed-in account to verify your identity.

### Link the OTP App and Verify the Code

1. In the "**Enable 2FA**" pop-up, click "**Next**". The system will generate a QR code.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-28/b6c2e31d8d6a44548898358cdbb6b733.webp)

2. Open the OTP app on your phone (Microsoft Authenticator is used as an example), select "**Add account**" > "**Scan QR code**", and scan the QR code generated by UGOS Pro.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-28/1ba2d7483eca411bbb1157fad0c3adb6.webp)

3. After the account is successfully linked, the OTP app will add a UGREEN account and start generating 6-digit one-time password codes.

4. Enter the current 6-digit verification code displayed in the OTP app on your phone, then click "**Next**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-28/c7a667c6a8bd414c986f0994bf1992b4.webp)

### Set an Emergency Verification Email Address (Optional)

1. Enter an emergency email address and click "**Send**" to get a verification code.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-28/7facc5b5d0894412aa5ed09f9be18086.webp)

2. Check your email for the verification code, enter the code, and submit it to complete the 2FA setup.

**Note**: The emergency email address is used to restore access to your account if you are unable to obtain an OTP code. Make sure to use an email address that you regularly use and that is secure.

### Login Process After Enabling 2FA

After enabling two-factor authentication, complete the following steps the next time you sign in to UGOS Pro:

1. Enter your account and password, then click "**Sign in**".

2. On the verification code screen, open the OTP app on your phone, enter the current 6-digit verification code, and click "**Sign in**" to complete the login.

### Manage Trusted Devices

Select "**Trust the current equipment**" on the sign-in screen to skip 2FA verification on subsequent sign-ins.

**View and Revoke Trusted Devices**:

1. Go to "**Local account settings**" > "**Account security**" > "**Manage trusted devices**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-28/13474e5583c04cec84ae8804f55eb31c.webp)

2. Enter the password for the signed-in account to verify your identity. You can then trust the current device or revoke trusted status from other devices.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-28/4105b8a248244550812356bd732c775e.webp)

**Quickly Trust the Current Device**

When signing in to UGOS Pro, select "**Trust the current equipment**" to add the current device to the trusted devices list.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-28/5ebbb6274c4f4cc3ba6f53deeb02ec00.webp)

## Notes

● Make sure the time on both UGOS Pro and your phone is set to synchronize automatically. Otherwise, OTP verification may fail.

● Keep your account password and OTP app secure to prevent unauthorized access caused by password leaks or device loss.

● The emergency email address is used to recover access to two-factor authentication. It is recommended to use an email address that you regularly use and that is secure. If you did not set an emergency email address when enabling 2FA for the first time, you need to disable 2FA and then enable it again to add or replace the email address.

● When signing in on an untrusted device, do not select "**Trust the current equipment**", and clear the browser cache promptly. Regularly review the list of trusted devices and revoke access for devices you no longer use.

● If your phone is lost or you are unable to obtain an OTP code, see "[How to Sign In to UGREEN NAS If Your Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Key Is Lost?](https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/detail/article/en-US/504?id=1479&type=tag002&clientType=PC)"
