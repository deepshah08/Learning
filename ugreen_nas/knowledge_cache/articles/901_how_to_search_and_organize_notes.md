## Introduction

When you have a large number of Notes, you can quickly find the content you need by using Search, Notes, and Section.

To keep your Notes organized, it's recommended to create separate Notebook and Section based on different use cases, such as Work Notes, Study Materials, Project Documents, and Personal Notes.

## How to Search Notes Content

Search is not currently supported.

## How to Quickly Find Notes in a Notebook

If you know which Notebook or Section the Notes belongs to, you can quickly locate it by navigating to the corresponding location in the left sidebar.

To do so:

1. Open the **Notes** app.

2. Select the target Notebook from the left sidebar.

3. Expand and select the corresponding Section.

4. View the Notes in the selected section from the middle pane.

Browsing by Notebook and Section helps narrow down the scope, making it easier to find the Notes you need.

## Does Notes Support Full-Text Search?

Full-text search is not currently supported.

## Does Notes Support Searching by Tags?

Searching by tags is not currently supported. We recommend organizing your Notes using Notebook, Section, and clear Titles for easier navigation.

## How to Organize a Large Number of Notes

You can organize your Notes using **Notebook+ Section+ Title**.

Here are some recommended ways to organize your Notes:

● By purpose: For example, Work Notes, Study Materials, Project Documents, and Personal Notes.

● By project: For example, Product Documentation, Shooting Scripts, Meeting Minutes, and Customer Information.

● By time: For example, 2026 Work Records, July Meeting Minutes, and Weekly Reviews.
