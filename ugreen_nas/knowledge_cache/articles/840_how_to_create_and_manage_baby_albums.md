# How to Create and Manage Baby Albums?

> **Article ID**: `840`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Photos > How to Create and Manage Baby Albums?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/840  

---

## Applicability

**Applicable Client**: UGREEN NAS PC Client, Web Browser

**Applicable Version**: UGOS Pro Firmware 1.17.0.0114 or later

The descriptions in this document are for reference only. The interface may vary depending on system or app updates, please refer to the actual interface.

## Introduction

**Baby Album** is a smart album designed to capture and organize your baby's growth. In addition to securely storing photos, it automatically organizes photos and creates milestones based on when the photos were taken and the baby's date of birth. You can also record information such as the baby's date of birth, gender, height, weight, and blood type. Baby Album also supports album sharing with family members, as well as comments and likes on photos, helping keep precious memories organized in one place.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/8771c700d30d4f29bffced29c46fe09e.webp)

**Note**: You can create a Baby Album by linking either a **baby profile photo** or a **folder containing the baby's photos**. If you link a baby profile photo when creating the album, the system will automatically recognize and organize photos of the baby from the Library, so you do not need to add them manually.

## Prerequisites

When creating a Baby Album for the first time, the People recognition model must be enabled.

**Method 1:** Download and authorize the required model in the "**Model Management**" app, and make sure the model is enabled. For details on downloading and authorizing models, see "[Model Management (DXP Series)](https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/detail/article/en-US/831)".

**Method 2:** On the home page, go to "**Settings**" > "**AI Settings**" and enable the "**People recognition**" model. If the model has not been downloaded, follow the on-screen instructions to download it.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/bfc0381dd1354557968923929f42ce77.webp)

## View People Categories

In the "**Photos**" sidebar, select "**Categorization**" > "**People**", then click "**Enable**". The system will begin recognizing faces in the Library. This may take some time, so please wait for the process to complete.

Once recognition is complete, you can view the results under the "**People**" tab. If any people are grouped incorrectly, you can manually adjust the groups, such as by merging, locking, or hiding them.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/736315849a7c43a6a0b19d24c6a2e67b.webp)

## Create a Baby Album

You can create a Baby Album in either of the following two ways.

### Method 1: Create by Linking a Baby Profile Photo

1. In the "**Photos**" app sidebar, click "**Personal Albums**".。

2. Click "**Create album**", select "**Baby album**", and enter an album name (for example, Baby Growth Journal).

3. Select your relationship to the baby (for example, Mom), or customize the relationship name.

4. Set the baby's basic information:

● Select a recognized baby profile photo.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/b1c1304e54ef48f7a7e3a58d44e1ffc0.webp)

● Enter the baby's nickname, and select the date of birth, gender, and other information.

● Add family members to the Baby Album, define each user's relationship to the baby (for example, Dad), and set the corresponding album access permission (for example, Manage).

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/a4f3ed9ee1714128ae32ce9620a6d4db.webp)

5. Click "**Create album**" to finish. The system will automatically organize photos based on the linked baby profile photo, photo capture date, and the baby's date of birth.

### Method 2: Create by Linking a Folder

If you want to create a Baby Album by linking a folder that contains the baby's photos, select the corresponding Shared Folder or Personal Folder on the Baby Album creation page.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/96838b85d8ef4ec1bba23a5b8a711a36.webp)

The system will automatically organize photos from the linked folder based on the photo capture date and the baby's date of birth. Duplicate photos in the same path will be automatically removed.

## View a Baby Album

After creating a Baby Album, you can view it under "**Personal Albums**". On the Baby Album details page, move the cursor to the right side of the page to quickly locate photos by the baby's growth timeline or by the year the photos were taken.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/c83283ac760d48998ffc3c26cc6a6f9c.webp)

In the baby's age timeline view, you can browse photos based on the baby's date of birth, and like or comment on photos uploaded by family members.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/be444657e63a495eb23fe5abae6638f9.webp)

From the menu bar at the top of the album, you can filter photos to view only those uploaded by you or those uploaded by others.。

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/35b7cbe96c3d4fe7aa4726178254e184.webp)

## Manage a Baby Album

On the Baby Album details page, you can manage the album in various ways as described below：

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/868ce8c79963443883c7ed364d518da2.webp)

### Add Family Members and Set Sharing Permissions

On the Baby Album details page, you can grant access to other NAS users, such as family members, and invite them to manage the album together.

1. Click the "**+**" icon on the page. In the Add family member pop-up, click "**Add user**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/d287dffd0ba74808a2a92b5594ab8c86.webp)

2. You can add multiple users at once. After selecting the users, click "**Confirm**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/edb2aec5e56b4802b047207e625aa9ce.webp)

3. Set permissions for the added users. You can apply permissions in batches or set permissions individually for each user.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/beb6c8604674464590d4adea43740e5e.webp)

### Invite Friends and Family (Non-NAS Users) to View the Album

The recipient does not need to download the client or sign in to a NAS account. You can create an external sharing link to invite friends and family to view the Baby Album.

1. Click the "**Share link**" icon. In the "**Edit external sharing**" pop-up, set the access permission (View only / Download), validity period, access password, and whether to allow access to photo details.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/3e01595a139f46aca483014f3b592f33.webp)

2. After completing the settings, click "**Copy link**" or download the QR code.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/c0af7b748cac44f1839470b28c2b66e0.webp)

3. Share the link or QR code with friends and family. They can click the shared link or scan the QR code to view the baby's photos.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/59dcdea703a149cb925e0affecad47da.webp)

### Invite Friends and Family to Upload Baby Photos

The recipient does not need to download the client or sign in to a NAS account. You can use the "**Invite friends to upload**" feature to allow friends and family without a NAS account to upload baby photos to the album.

1. On the Baby Album details page, click "**Invite friends to upload**" from the "**...**" menu.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/ae129c9f9dc240e19355aec6275c2462.webp)

2. In the "**Invite friends to upload**" pop-up, set the invitation name and note, select the storage location, and configure the validity period and access password.

3. After creating the invitation, click "**Copy link**" or download the QR code.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/a34a65f415cc4ad9be6cdfe15edc46b1.webp)

4. Share the invitation link or QR code with friends and family. They can click the link or scan the QR code to upload baby photos from their phone or local device.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/8e17ec3be33840baa62517601d47fca9.webp)

### More Actions

● **Manually upload photos**: Click the "**Upload photos**" icon to manually add photos that were not automatically recognized from your local device or existing Library.

● **Download or delete**: Click the "**...**" icon to download the entire album to your local device for backup or delete the album. Deleting the album only removes the album classification. The original photos remain safely stored in the Library and folders.

● **Rename the album**: Click "**Rename**" or click the album name at the top of the page to rename it.

● **Edit baby information**: Click the "**...**" icon and select "**Edit baby album**". You can add baby profile photos and folders, and update information such as height, weight, and blood type at any time to keep a complete growth record.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/e4cc38ae5552497cbcdf905dc1a54103.webp)

● **Play as slideshow**: Enter slideshow mode to configure imageduration, playback mode, display aspect ratio, and music settings.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/9942da12fd5c4d478a6c52218bdefd3f.webp)

● **Add to sidebar**: From the "**...**" menu, add the Baby Album to the custom sidebar for quick access.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/d80399dc0ca94d50bb18ee53b53297d4.webp)

● **Stop sharing**: If the album has been shared internally or through a sharing link, you can stop sharing it at any time. Other users will no longer be able to access the album.

● **Change the album cover**: Select a photo in the Baby Album, then choose "**Set as cover**" from the "**...**" menu to set it as the album cover.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/e5721b6646734dac8cc1c469d7603f55.webp)

● **Other actions**: After selecting one or more photos in the album, you can also add them to People, enable Set privacy protection, add them to Favorites, adjust the recording time, delete them, or remove the selected photos from the album.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/2d3515951bb246fe86bd40707a058536.webp)

## View or Create Baby Milestones

**Baby Album Milestones** are photo collections that capture important milestones in your baby's growth, such as birth and the one-month milestone, as well as special moments such as taking the first steps or going on the first trip. The system can **automatically generate** milestones based on the baby's date of birth, and you can also **create milestones manually**, helping you keep precious moments from your baby's growth organized.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-08-21/1c922bbbb51b459ca4738542633b8386.webp)

For more details, see "[How to Create Baby Album Milestones?](https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/detail/article/en-US/913?clientType=PC)"
