# How to Restore Accidentally Deleted Photos in the Photos App?

> **Article ID**: `574`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Photos > FAQ > How to Restore Accidentally Deleted Photos in the Photos App?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/574  

---

**Applicable models**: DH series, DX series, DXP series, and iDX series

**Applicable clients**: UGREEN NAS PC client and web browser

**Applicable version**: UGOS Pro firmware 1.16.0.0042 and later

The screenshots in this article are for reference only. The actual interface may vary slightly depending on the system or app version. Please refer to the actual interface.

## Scenario Description

If you accidentally delete photos or videos in Photos, you can restore them from "**Recycle Bin**". All items deleted from Library or Folder are moved to "**Recycle Bin**" first, so you can easily recover them later.

## Steps

### Open Recycle Bin

On PC, you can open "Recycle Bin" in either of the following ways:

● **Desktop shortcut:** Click the **"Recycle Bin**" icon on the NAS system desktop.

● **In-app entry:** Open the **"Files**" app. At the bottom of the sidebar, click the **"Recycle Bin**" icon.

### Find and Restore Photos

1. In "**Recycle Bin**", select the folder category where the deleted photo was originally stored.

● **Personal:** Files deleted from your "**Personal folder**".

● **Shared:** Files deleted from "**Shared folder**".

● **Users**: Visible to administrators only. Files deleted from other users' "**Personal folder**" on this device.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-25/aaaf7201baab4076ab3773cc383d1bb0.webp)

2. In the corresponding category, find the photo you want to restore. You can sort by "**Name**", "**Deletion time**", and other options, or use "**Search**" to quickly locate it.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-25/1668680629864151bd41e48d8a95d692.webp)

3. Hold down Shift or Ctrl to select the photos you want to restore. Multiple selections are supported. Then right-click the selected photos and select "**Restore**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-05-25/003c524ced684ce6842158b579dc85f9.webp)

4. After the system shows "**Operated successfully**", the photos will be automatically restored to their original storage location and appear in "**Library**" again.

## Additional Notes

● Regular users can only view and restore files under the "**Personal**" category. Restore permissions for shared folders are set by the administrator.

● Files in "Recycle Bin" are usually kept permanently. If the administrator has set a scheduled cleanup plan, they will be automatically and permanently deleted. Please restore accidentally deleted items in time.
