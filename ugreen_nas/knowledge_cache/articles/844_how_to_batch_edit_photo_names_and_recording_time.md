> Screenshots and interfaces in this document are for reference only. Actual displays may vary depending on system or application version. Some features may change across versions, please refer to your actual interface.

On the photo detail page, you can quickly edit a photo's name and shooting time. Batch editing for both name and recording time is also supported.

## Edit Name and Recording Time for a Single Photo

1. On the "**Library**" page, select a photo to open the detail view, then click the "**Details**" icon.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-30/ef895164be274870a80c79d75f1359d4.webp)

2. In the photo information panel, locate the "**name**" and "**recording time**" fields, and click the edit icon.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-30/179a2fb8cd1d4214b65c7c5fbc4f24c4.webp)

3. Enter the new name, and edit recording time, then click "**Confirm**" to save automatically.

## Batch Rename Photos

1. Open "**Files**" app, hold **Shift** or **Ctrl**, and select multiple photos. Then click the "**Rename**" icon.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-30/47461c3a61f7409799fc7b3b242ec29e.webp)

2. In the "**Batch rename**" dialog, choose one of the following methods:

● **Find/Replace**: Replace keywords in file names or extensions in bulk.

● **Number format**: Add sequence numbers, prefixes, or suffixes to make file names more organized.

● **Add content**: Insert text or numbering at the beginning or end of file names.

● **Edit manually**: Customize names freely by editing them one by one.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-30/7631856b83c841099af42b479ce3c1b9.webp)

3. After configuring the naming method, click "**Rename**" to apply changes.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-30/3148e796bbbc48569a2b1d965ec26398.webp)

## Batch Edit Recording Time

1. On the "**Library**" page, select multiple photos, click the "**...**" icon in the bottom toolbar, and choose "**Adjust recording time**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-30/7eeaf8b10c5142a1bc76d4034dfe32ba.webp)

2. In the dialog, click the "**Select time**" icon to modify the date and time (the option to preserve relative time differences is enabled by default).

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-04-30/1d593277c78042149379506de53354fa.webp)

3. Click "**Confirm**" to apply changes in bulk. The photos will be re-sorted in the "Library" based on the updated time.
