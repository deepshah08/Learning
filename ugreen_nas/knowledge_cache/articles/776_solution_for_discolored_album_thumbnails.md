# Solution for Discolored Album Thumbnails

> **Article ID**: `776`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Photos > FAQ > Solution for Discolored Album Thumbnails`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `COMMON`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/776  

---

**Q:** Why do NAS album thumbnails appear yellowish or discolored on the app, while the original images display correctly?

## Cause Analysis

This issue is usually caused by abnormal thumbnail cache data in the mobile app, leading to color parsing errors. Thumbnails are temporary cache files generated by the client to speed up previews, and any color distortion in thumbnails does not affect the quality of the original photos.

## Solution

To resolve this issue, clear the mobile app cache and rebuild the thumbnails by following these steps:

1. Open the UGREEN NAS mobile app and log in. From the home page, tap the user avatar in the top-left corner to open the sidebar, then go to "**General"** to access the general settings.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-12-24/0702337c67dc4d0b98656736d6772bac.webp)

2. Clear the cache:

● **iOS Devices:** In "**General**", tap "**Clear all caches**". A confirmation prompt will appear; tap "**Confirm**" to automatically clear the cache.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-12-24/03f56ec96c234cc5bf410d25f7353189.webp)

● **Android Devices:** In "**General**", go to **Cache Management** > **Clear Cache**. A confirmation prompt will appear; tap **Confirm** to automatically clear the cache.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2025-12-24/07c6680cd1044c96bb84792ff612b7bf.webp)

After clearing the cache, log out of the app and log back in. The system will regenerate the thumbnails, which should now display with the correct colors.
