# How to Reset to Factory Settings

> **Article ID**: `325`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Control Panel > FAQ > How to Reset to Factory Settings`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `COMMON`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/325

## Introduction

If you need to reset your UGREEN NAS to its factory settings, you can do so via the **Control Panel** or the **RESET** button on the device.

Resetting to factory settings will erase all system settings and installed applications, including Docker containers and virtual machines. Personal data and files stored on the hard drives will not be deleted.

To prevent unexpected data loss, it is recommended to back up important data to another secure location before proceeding.

## Resetting to Factory Settings Using the RESET Button

You can reset the device to its factory settings using the **RESET** button. Follow the steps below:

1. Ensure the NAS is powered on.

2. Use a pin tool to press and hold the **RESET** button on the device for about **5 seconds**.

3. After you hear one short beep, continue holding the button for approximately **8 seconds** without releasing it.

4. When you hear three short beeps, release the button. The device will automatically restart and begin the reset process.

5. When the network indicator turns solid white, the device is ready for normal use.

After the reset is complete, you can bind and initialize the device again.

**Note**: During the process, make sure to release the RESET button only after hearing three short beeps.

If you release the button after only one short beep, the reset process will not be triggered.

## Resetting to Factory Settings via the Control Panel

You can also reset the device to its factory settings from the system desktop through the **Control Panel**. Follow the steps below:

1. Log in to the system desktop, open the **Control Panel**, **and go to** "**Update & Restore**" **>** "**Restore & Reset**".

2. Locate the Factory reset option and click "**Reset to factory settings**" on the right.

3. In the pop-up window, select "**I accept**" and click "**Reset**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-07-06/efd1d5e0035d4af69d27d2b5d4d7ed0a.webp)

4. Enter your account password as required and click "**Send**" to complete identity verification.

After verification is passed, the system will begin the factory reset process.

## Notes

● During the reset process, keep the device powered on. Do not manually power off or restart the device.

● Resetting to factory settings will erase all system settings and all installed applications, including Docker containers and virtual machines.

● Resetting to factory settings will not delete personal data or files stored on the hard drives.

● To avoid data loss, it is recommended to manually back up important data to another secure location before proceeding, such as an external hard drive or another NAS device.
