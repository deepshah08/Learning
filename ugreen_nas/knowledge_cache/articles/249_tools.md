## Sharing Management

The album supports sharing photos and album files externally. To share a photo or album, follow the steps below:

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20241220/f20430d9-f697-4a0a-8292-0d63cd03ba4e.png)

1. Go to the **Album**, find the album you want to share, hover over the album frame, click the "`···`" button in the top right corner >**Share** and enter the share creation wizard. (For sharing a single photo file, find the corresponding photo, select it, and click the "**share**" **icon** to enter the **Create share** wizard.)
2. You can choose the share type. If you select **Public Sharing**, the system will generate a share link. You can share this link with friends, and they can open the link to browse the shared album content. You can also set the share expiration, access password, and download permissions. (Note: Public sharing requires enabling the UGlink service first.)
3. If you select **share to internal users only**, you can share the album with users or user groups on the device, setting view permissions and upload permissions. Other users can view the shared album in the **Album** options.

**To Manage Sharing**

You can browse the created share tasks in **Tool > Sharing Management** by following these steps:

* You can view the created share tasks here, including shared content, creation date, task expiration, details of users for internal sharing, and the link generated for public sharing.
* Click the "**edit**“ button on any task to re-edit the share task.

## **Friends Upload Management**

The album supports **inviting friends to upload photos**. To invite friends to upload, follow the steps below:

**Method 1**:

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20241220/aac8ee7a-590e-4b34-9bb3-94d1578e1edb.png)

1. Go to the **Album**, find the regular or baby album where you intend to invite uploads. Hover over the album frame, **click the "···" button in the top right corner** > **Invite Friends to Upload**, and enter the invite upload task wizard.
2. On the wizard page, you can **set the task name for the upload task**, **the information displayed to friends on the upload page**, and **the Validity period**. You can also modify the path in the personal library and set upload limit (e.g.,single file size limit, access password for the task).
3. Click "**Apply**" to generate the invite upload task link. You can send the link to your friends, and once they open it, they can upload files to the folder. When uploading files with the same name, the files will be automatically renamed.

**Method 2**:

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20241220/0937d3c8-e629-4b6e-be89-6ddbcf6d8c42.png)

1. Go to the **Album** option, open the manually created baby album or regular album, click the "`+`" icon in the top right corner > **Invite Friends to Upload Photos**, and enter the invite upload task wizard.
2. On the wizard page, you can set the task name for the upload invitation, the information displayed to friends on the upload page, and the task expiration. You can also modify the save path in the personal library and set upload limit (e.g., single file size limit, access password for the task).
3. Click "**Apply"** to generate the invite upload task link. You can send the link to your friends, and after they open it, they can upload files to the folder. When uploading files with the same name, the files will be automatically renamed.

**To manage friend-upload tasks,**

You can browse the created friend upload tasks in **Tool > Friend Upload Management** by following these steps:

* You can view the created friend upload tasks here, including uploaded file content, creation date, task expiration, and the file save path for the upload task. Click the edit button on any task to re-edit the upload task.
* To clear expired tasks, click the "**Clear Expired Links**" **icon** in the top right corner. The system will clean all expired links with one click.

## View Similar or Duplicate Photos

With this feature, you can easily filter out duplicate or similar photos to optimize storage space and photo management. Here are the specific steps to use it:

1. **Enable Similar/Duplicate Photo Recognition Model**  
   Before using this feature, you need to download and enable the intelligent recognition model:

* Click the **User Icon > Settings > AI Settings** in the top right corner.
* Find the "**Similar/Duplicate Photo Recognition**" download it, and enable it.

1. **Access the Similar or Duplicate Photo Tool**

* Go to **Tools > Search ofr similar or duplicate photos** and perform the following actions:

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20241220/ea3f9556-291a-4a6d-b178-e0cb20134ba1.png)

* Click the "**Search for Duplicate Photos"** or "**Search for Similar Photos"** button.

* Select the range of photos to search (the default is the personal photo library).

* Click "**Confirm**", and the system will begin creating the search task.

3. **Manage and Delete Results**

* **View Results**: After the search is complete, click the task to view the details, including the grouped display of duplicate or similar photos.
* **Delete Photos**: Select the photos you want to delete, and click the "**Delete"** icon in the action bar to free up storage space.

**Note**: If the Recycle Bin is not enabled for the photo directory, deleted photos will be permanently lost and cannot be restored.
