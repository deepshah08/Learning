# Log in UGOS Pro System

> **Article ID**: `60`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > UGOS Pro > Log in UGOS Pro System`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `COMMON`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/60  

---

**Applicable Models**: DH Series, DX Series, DXP Series, iDX Series

**Applicable Version**: UGOS Pro firmware 1.18.0.0076 and higher

The screenshots in this document are for reference only. The actual interface may vary slightly depending on the system or app version, please refer to the actual interface displayed.

After successfully creating an administrator account and enabling the UGREENlink remote access service, you can log in to and access the UGOS Pro system through various terminal devices (such as a web browser, PC client, or mobile app). You can also choose to access your NAS device through a local network login or remote login based on your network environment.

## LAN Login

When the NAS device and computer are connected to the same local area network, you can log in to the UGOS Pro system using the following methods.

### Method 1: Log in via a browser

**Find the NAS device through**[**find.ugnas.com**](https://find.ugnas.com/#/)

1. Enter [find.ugnas.com](http://find.ugnas.com) in the browser address bar. The system will automatically scan for available NAS devices on the local network.

2. Select your NAS from the device list and click "**Connect**".

3. Enter the NAS account and password on the login page, then click "**Log in**" to access the UGOS Pro system.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-03/e962b3648237473a9afbfa82a866bb5e.webp)

**Access directly using the NAS IP address**

1. Enter the NAS IP address and port number in the browser address bar, for example: `http://192.168.31.70:9999`

2. Enter the NAS account and password on the login page, then click "**Log in**" to access the UGOS Pro system.

**Note**: To obtain the NAS IP address, go to "**Control Panel**" **>** "**Network Settings**" **>** "**Network Connection**".

### Method 2: Log in using the UGREEN NAS PC Client or Mobile App

1. Visit the UGREEN NAS official website and go to the [Download Center](https://ai.ugreen.com/?no_rule=true). Download and install the UGREEN NAS PC Client or mobile app compatible with your NAS model.

2. Open the UGREEN NAS app client. On the login page, enter the NAS IP address or UGREENlink ID, along with the NAS account and password.

3. Click "**Login**" to access the UGOS Pro system.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-03/63b4567009b64a21bd17974327f7e72c.webp)

## Remote Login

The UGOS Pro system supports the UGREENlink remote access service, allowing users to access their NAS devices anytime and anywhere through a mobile device, computer, or web browser. When your NAS and the accessing device are not connected to the same local area network, you can use the UGREENlink remote access feature to log in from the internet.

**Copy the remote access address**:

1. Log in to the system desktop, open the "**Control Panel**" app, and go to "**Device Connection**" **>** "**Remote access**".

2. Set a UGREENlink ID (for example, `myNAS123`).

3. Copy the remote access address generated by the system, for example: https://ug.link/myNAS123

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-03/aa6b193f97b7406ab4c53a8c9c8a343d.webp)

### Method 1: Remote access via a browser

1. Enter the remote access link in the browser address bar on your computer or mobile device, for example:https://ug.link/myNAS123

2. Enter your NAS account and password on the login page, then click "**Log in**" to remotely access your NAS.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-03/399d253385804401a36533b4b4350928.webp)

### Method 2: Remote access using the PC or mobile app

1. Visit the UGREEN NAS official website and go to the [Download Center](https://ai.ugreen.com/?no_rule=true). Download and install the UGREEN NAS app client compatible with your NAS model.

2. Enter the UGREENlink ID (for example, `myNAS123`), NAS account, and password on the login page.

3. Click "**Login**" to remotely access your NAS.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-09-03/8870600a224540e3a74790a0bbe9a15c.webp)

## Notes

● To avoid entering the IP address, account, and password each time you log in, you can select "**Remember password**".

● If login fails, check whether the IP address, account, and password are entered correctly, or whether the device connection is working properly.

● If your NAS has an available public IPv4 address, you can bind a DDNS domain name for remote connection. For details, refer to "[How to Configure a DDNS Domain Name to Log In to UGREEN NAS?](https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/detail/article/zh-CN/862) "。
