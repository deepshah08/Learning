# How to Reset the Network Settings and Administrator Password on Your UGREEN NAS

> **Article ID**: `294`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Control Panel > FAQ > How to Reset the Network Settings and Administrator Password on Your UGREEN NAS`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/294  

---

## Procedure

1. Ensure that the NAS device is powered on.

2. Use a pin tool to press and hold the **RESET** button on the device for approximately **5** seconds.

3. Release the button after you hear a short beep from the device.

4. The device will automatically restart and begin resetting the network settings and password.

5. Once the reset is complete, the network status LED will remain solid white, indicating that the device is ready for use.

## Logging In After Reset

After the reset is complete, use the following credentials to sign in to the system:

● Username: **admin**

● Password: (leave blank)

This account is intended for password reset only.

After signing in, follow the on-screen setup wizard to create a new administrator password.

## Notes

Resetting the network settings and administrator password does not affect the data stored on the hard drives.
