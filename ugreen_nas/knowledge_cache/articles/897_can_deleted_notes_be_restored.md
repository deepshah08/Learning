# Can Deleted Notes Be Restored

> **Article ID**: `897`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Notes > Can Deleted Notes Be Restored`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/897  

---

## Introduction

The current version does not support restoring deleted Notes from the Notes App. Before deleting a Note, make sure it is no longer needed.

## Delete a Single Note

Follow the steps below:

1. Open the **Notes** App and find the Note you want to delete from the middle list.

2. Hover over the Note, click "**···**" on the right, and select "**Delete**".

3. Follow the instructions on the page to confirm the deletion.

You can also open the Note, click "**···**" at the top of the editing area on the right, and select "**Delete**".

## Delete Notes in Batch

Follow the steps below:

1. Click "**Batch select**" at the top of the middle list, and select the Notes you want to delete.

2. Click "**Delete**" in the operation bar at the bottom of the page.

3. Follow the instructions on the page to confirm the deletion.

## Notes

Deleted Notes cannot be restored. It is recommended that you export important Notes before deleting them.

To keep important content for long-term storage, you can export Notes as PDF or Markdown files and save them to your local computer, NAS file system, or another secure location.
