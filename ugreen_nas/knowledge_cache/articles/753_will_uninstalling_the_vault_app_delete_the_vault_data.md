# Will uninstalling the Vault app delete the Vault data?

> **Article ID**: `753`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Vault > Will uninstalling the Vault app delete the Vault data?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/753  

---

## Issue Description

Will the data in the Vault be deleted or lost after uninstalling the "Vault" app on UGREEN NAS?

## Cause Analysis

Uninstalling an application only removes its program files and does not delete the data it has generated or used. Vault data is stored in an encrypted folder at a designated location on the hard drive and does not depend on the app's presence. Even after the app is uninstalled, the data remains intact.

## Solution

● Uninstalling the Vault app will not delete the data stored in the Vault.

● After reinstalling the app, you can remount and access the original data simply by entering the original password.

● If you’re concerned about data security, it’s recommended to back up related information and keep the password safe before uninstalling.
