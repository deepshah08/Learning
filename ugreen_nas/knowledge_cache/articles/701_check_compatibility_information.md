# Check Compatibility Information

> **Article ID**: `701`  
> **Category**: `Troubleshooting > Product Compatibility List > Check Compatibility Information`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `COMMON`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/701  

---

To ensure system stability and optimal performance, it is recommended to select hardware products that have passed the official UGREEN compatibility tests when configuring your NAS.

For the compatibility lists of various hardware accessories for UGREEN NAS **DXP and DH series** devices, please refer to the [UGREEN NAS Products Compatibility List](https://nas.ugreen.com/pages/compatibility) to check the latest supported models and detailed support information.

**Notes:**

● Please choose hard drives, memory modules, UPS devices, and other accessories that have passed compatibility testing to achieve better performance and user experience.

● Compatibility testing cannot cover all models on the market or the changes brought by hardware and software updates; future updates may affect device compatibility and stability.

● Both HDDs and SSDs have limited lifespans. Regularly check the health status of your drives and back up your data to prevent data loss due to drive failure.

● SMR drives may exhibit unstable performance under continuous write operations or in RAID environments, potentially causing array degradation or data loss. Avoid using Shingled Magnetic Recording (SMR) drives.

● Mixing new and old drives in a RAID array is not recommended, as differences in performance or lifespan may lead to array errors or data inconsistency.
