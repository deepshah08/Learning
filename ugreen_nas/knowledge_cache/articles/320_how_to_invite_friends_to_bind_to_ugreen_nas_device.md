# How to Invite Friends to Bind to UGREEN NAS Device

> **Article ID**: `320`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Control Panel > FAQ > How to Invite Friends to Bind to UGREEN NAS Device`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/320  

---

If you wish to use the UGREEN NAS device with your friends, you can do so by sending an invitation.

By reading this content, you will learn how to invite users to join via QR code invitation and link invitation in [Control Panel]>[User Management]>[Add].

## **Introduction to User Management**

The user management function can greatly simplify the management of user accounts, improve work efficiency, and ensure the security of data and the effectiveness of user access permissions.

## **Operation Steps Explanation**

**Step1: QR Code Invitation**

1. Send the QR code to your friends, they scan the code to register the UGREEN Account, fill in the basic account information, and click "Apply to Join".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250804/eb948b4f-a20c-43f1-8f00-388e45edb13a.png)

2. After receiving the application, the administrator approves it and sets permissions such as the role and user group for this user.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250804/2a0ac97b-e9d1-4639-87ee-f73050805213.png)

3. Click "Complete", and your friend can successfully join and use the UGREEN NAS device.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250804/81da0565-2f4b-4fd6-b3bc-59cffd3b1865.png)

**Step2: Link Invitation**

1. Copy the invitation link and send it to your friend, your friend clicks the link to fill in the account information and applies to join.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250804/1f4c2d05-961b-4cf0-b242-ec556a2d049f.png)

2. After receiving the application, the administrator approves it and sets permissions such as the role and user group for this user.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250804/0076284d-a353-42db-8a83-a1d7d51ece9f.png)

3. Click "Complete", and your friend can successfully join and use the UGREEN NAS device.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250804/7868c323-63a2-4444-b946-dd4a17bc907c.png)

Through the above steps, you can easily invite friends to join, use the UGREEN NAS device together, and enjoy a convenient user management experience.
