# UGREEN NAS Theater Collection Feature: Unlock a Personalized Movie Management Experience

> **Article ID**: `650`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Theater > FAQ > UGREEN NAS Theater Collection Feature: Unlock a Personalized Movie Management Experience`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/650  

---

In the UGREEN NAS Theater, the Collection feature offers a convenient and personalized way to manage movie and TV resources. Users can easily organize and browse their media, enjoying a smoother and more enjoyable viewing experience.

Leveraging the Collection recognition technology in the Theater, the system automatically handles:

* Smart aggregation of movie series (e.g., *Avengers* series, *Iron Man* series)
* Seasonal categorization of TV series (e.g., *Game of Thrones* S1-S8 independent collections, prequel collections)

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250427/6864da50-3cb1-4ce3-9fa6-d47118b06ee4.png)

When the collection cannot be correctly identified, the Theater offers solutions such as manual identification and metadata editing

* Manual Identification: Match the correct collection through the database.
* Edit Metadata: Edit basic information such as cover images, background images, titles, years, and categories.

## **How to View the Collection List**

To view the list of created collections, go to the left sidebar navigation menu on the Theater homepage and click the "Collection" button to see all collections. If you only want to view collections from a specific library, click the filter button within that library, select "Collection" as the filter type, and you can quickly locate the desired collection.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250427/0d73717c-42cd-4473-9a8e-236ab47a5694.png)

## **Manually Create a Collection**

If a collection doesn't have a poster cover, users can manually add different movies to a custom collection based on their preferences and needs. For example, users can add all works of a favorite actor into a "[Actor's Name] Collection," or group films of a specific genre, such as "Historical Fantasy Drama Collection," "Mystery Thriller Movie Collection," or "Marvel Series," to create a personalized movie collection.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250427/e2107593-0d78-4c9d-88dc-85fded8a24ed.png)

### **Manually Add to a Collection**

To add movies, TV shows, or other films to a collection or manually create a new collection, follow these steps:

1. Select the film and hover the mouse over the film's poster card, then click the "..." button.
2. In the pop-up options, choose "Add to Collection."
3. You can add the film to an existing collection by selecting the collection and clicking "Confirm" to add it.
4. If there isn't an appropriate collection, click "Create New Collection," enter a collection name, and press "Enter" to create it.
5. Once created, select the collection and click "Confirm" to add the film to the collection.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250427/cc4112ab-515d-4de4-8037-58f92af874c9.png)

6. Once the collection is created, you can view it in the library interface or click the "Filter" button in the top right corner of the library to filter and view the collection.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250427/07d6569b-d50b-459c-9256-05b8bd3b334d.png)

## **Personalized Poster Settings**

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250427/440440fb-5aba-4bb2-99d7-4d4fa3f24559.png)

1. When you encounter a collection with a name but no poster cover, you can click the collection to enter the detail page, then click the "···" and find the "Manual Identification" option.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250427/bd7cd6ce-8438-4467-85ae-82db7c85e5fd.png)

2. Then, enter the collection name to search, and the system will intelligently match the appropriate collection series poster cover for you.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250427/e5a79091-8a91-408f-bdbc-cc25c8e62426.png)

3. If it's a manually created collection and the appropriate poster can't be matched through manual identification, you can manually upload a poster cover and edit related information. On the collection's detail page, click the "···" and select "Edit Metadata."

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250427/9ce5458a-fde8-4a92-8851-93371c92ecc3.png)

4. Upload the carefully selected poster cover image, then click "Save" to give the collection a unique appearance.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/ugreen-pro/admin/article/20250427/311de30b-c2bc-4374-9a2d-e2c19768185f.png)

## **Manage Collections**

After creating a collection, you can perform the following management operations at any time:

* Dissolve Collection: Hover over the collection poster card, click the "..." button, and select "Dissolve Collection." Dissolving the collection will not delete the user's video files, scraped NFOs, or video poster images.
* Rename Collection: Select the collection you want to modify, right-click and choose "Rename."
* Edit Metadata: To modify the collection's poster cover and description, click "Edit Metadata" to make changes.
* Manual Identification: Collections support scraping for posters and descriptions, with manual identification functionality. Right-click the collection, select "Manual Identification," enter the name, and search for matching information.
