# How to Play Theater Videos Offline

> **Article ID**: `812`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Theater > How to Play Theater Videos Offline`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `MOBILE`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/812  

---

When traveling without internet access, or to avoid excessive mobile data usage, you can download videos from Theater app to your phone or tablet in advance for offline viewing anytime.

## Device and Version Requirements

To ensure the offline download feature works properly, make sure your mobile device and NAS meet the following requirements:

● **UGREEN NAS firmware**: Version 1.14.10 or later.

● **iOS devices**: UGREEN NAS app version V1.13.0 or later.

● **Android devices**: UGREEN NAS app version V1.14.0 or later

## Download Videos to Local Device

1. In "**Theater**", open the details page of the target video. Tap the "**···**" icon in the top-right corner, then select "**Download**" from the bottom menu.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-03-30/1f8e6a5140414270888c4d1b71a9c32f.webp)

2. On the download configuration page, tap the "**Download**" button next to a specific episode, or tap "**Download all**" at the bottom to start the download task.

**Notes**:

● Tap "**Definition**" in the top-right corner to select the desired video resolution (e.g., Original, 1080P).

● If the selected quality is not available, the system will automatically download the highest available resolution.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-03-30/d365061afea7436da7d03065ceb52f8b.webp)

## Check Download Progress

You can monitor the download status in real time using either of the following methods:

● **Quick access:** On the download configuration page, tap "**View progress**" in the bottom-left corner.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-03-30/e8fdc3303c0f4d33aecda15d14f87e83.webp)

● **Global access:** On the Theater homepage, tap "**Settings**" in the bottom menu, then select "**My downloads**" to view progress.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-03-30/21e3b027536b4fcd98d43ff9aefe0094.webp)

## Play and Access Offline Videos

The method to access offline videos varies depending on your device's current network status:

### When Connected to the Internet (Able to Log In)

1. Open the app and log in. On the homepage, tap your profile avatar in the top-left corner. In the expanded sidebar, select "**Offline data**".

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-03-30/e4e74cedc8dd4031b83ce1de1d6ff1ee.webp)

2. Choose "**Theater**" to view and play offline videos.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-03-30/335273959e9c4cde8af7434fb9429e1a.webp)

### When Offline (Unable to Log In)

Open the app and remain on the login page. Tap "**Offline Data**" at the bottom, then select "**Theater**" to access and play offline videos.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-03-30/0f1640b61fca4e6d98bae8a498b4e563.webp)

### For Users with Biometric Login Enabled

If the device triggers a Face ID or fingerprint verification prompt while offline, follow these steps:

1. Tap "**More**" option on the page. From the bottom menu, select "**Another account**" to return to the standard login page.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-03-30/36bc1433e8e541878ce0ea683d576ced.webp)

2. On the login page, tap "**Offline Data**" at the bottom, then select "**Theater**" to view and play offline videos.

![](https://file-us.ugreennas.com/admin/article/2026-03-30/1c23f0c334d84997a9aa03a4ce5907cf.webp)
