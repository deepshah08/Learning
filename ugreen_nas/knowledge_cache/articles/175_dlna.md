# DLNA

> **Article ID**: `175`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > DLNA > DLNA`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/175  

---

DLNA is a standardized technology protocol designed for sharing digital media and content services within a home network. NAS devices typically provide DLNA services, allowing you to access and stream media files stored on the NAS using DLNA-compatible devices such as smart TVs, audio systems, and computers within the local network.

Once **DLNA** is enabled, supported devices—including TVs, computers, and TV boxes—can access media files (such as music, videos, and images) stored on the NAS within the local network.

The **DLNA service** supports various common media formats and features automatic discovery, ensuring seamless access and synchronized playback control across multiple devices.

**Note:**

When DLNA is enabled, all added media resources will be accessible to other devices on the local network without authentication. To protect your privacy, exercise caution when adding personal folders or sensitive files.
