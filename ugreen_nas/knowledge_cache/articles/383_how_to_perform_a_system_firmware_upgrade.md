# How to Perform a System Firmware Upgrade?

> **Article ID**: `383`  
> **Category**: `Application Guide > Control Panel > FAQ > How to Perform a System Firmware Upgrade?`  
> **Client Compatibility**: `PC`  
> **Official URL**: https://support.ugnas.com/knowledgecenter/#/detail/383  

---

**This article introduces how to upgrade UGREEN NAS system firmware on both PC and mobile clients.**

### **Updating System Firmware on PC Client**

**Method 1: Automatic Update**

1. Open the PC client and go to [Control Panel] > [Update & Restore] >[System Update].
2. Click the [Update Now] button.
3. Confirm the operation, and the system will begin the update.

**Note:** During the system update, all application services will be stopped.

**Method 2: Manual Update**

1. Visit the [UGREEN NAS official website] > [Download Center].
2. Choose your device model and download the required system firmware package.
3. Return to the PC client and open [Control Panel] > [Update & Restore] >[System Update].
4. Click [Manual Installation], then select the downloaded firmware file and click "OK" to begin the installation.

### **Updating System Firmware on Mobile Client**

1. Log in to the mobile client app.
2. If a new version is available, an upgrade notification will appear on the home screen. Tap [Update] to begin the process.
3. If you ignored the home screen notification, go to [Control Panel] > [Update & Restore] to check for updates and complete the upgrade.
