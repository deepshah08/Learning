## 🚀 How to Download (Step-by-Step)

Because this archive is preserved on an offline, energy-saving mechanical cold storage drive, downloads are activated on-demand.

### 1. Requesting the Link
Whenever you are ready to download (make sure you have an uninterrupted Wi-Fi / wired internet connection and at least 65 GB of free space on your computer or external drive):

1. Ping Deep to activate your download window (or open your family download link).
2. Deep will send you your personal, encrypted download link:
   ```text
   https://pi5-media-nas.tail983cc9.ts.net:8089/df?token=<YOUR_SECURE_TOKEN>
   ```
3. This link will remain active for **4 hours**.

---

### 2. Downloading via Your Web Browser

1. Click the link in **Chrome**, **Safari**, **Edge**, or **Firefox** on your computer.
2. You will see the **Dhuri & Fenil Wedding Master Archive** card.
3. Click the blue **⬇️ Download Master Archive (60 GB)** button.
4. **Pause & Resume**: The server supports pause and resume. If your Wi-Fi flickers or drops, simply resume the download in your browser without starting over from 0%.

---

### 3. How to Open the Downloaded `.tar` File

Once downloaded, you have a single `.tar` archive containing all original folders and videos.

#### On Mac (macOS):
* Simply **double-click** `wedding_archive_DF.tar`.
* macOS Archive Utility will extract it into a folder named `DF/` containing `Photos/` and `Videos/`.

#### On Windows (Windows 10 / 11):
* Windows 11 natively supports `.tar` files. Right-click `wedding_archive_DF.tar` > **Extract All**.
* Alternatively, use [7-Zip](https://www.7-zip.org/) (free): Right-click > **7-Zip** > **Extract to "wedding_archive_DF"**.
